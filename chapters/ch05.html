Chapter 5: Additional Provisions for Regional Plan Areas

Master non-municipal territorial development across Maharashtra: the Gaothan Expansion Scheme (500m/1500m belts), 10% RP amenity surrender, committed development grandfathering, Konkan coastal wadi rules, Western Ghats high-altitude zones, and LIGO-India observatory buffers.

STATUTORY HIGHLIGHTS

Key Chapter Takeaways

  • Gaothan Expansion Belts (Reg. 5.1.1): 2.0 km around Municipal Corporations, 1.0 km around Municipal Councils, 500m for villages ≤ 5,000 population, 1,500m for villages > 5,000 population. 200m in Western Ghats. Payable @ 15% ASR land premium.
  • 50% Gut Rule (Reg. 5.1.1): If more than 50% of a revenue gut/survey number falls inside the expansion belt, the entire parcel is eligible for residential development. Personal owner houses exempt from premium.
  • RP Amenity Space (Reg. 5.1.8): Up to 4,000 sq.m: NIL. Above 4,000 sq.m: 10% amenity land surrender with 100% in-situ compensatory FSI or TDR.
  • Committed Development (Reg. 5.1.3): Approvals granted prior to draft RP publication remain legally valid in-toto, or can be revised under UDCPR without paying premium.
  • Railway Station Belt (Reg. 5.1.6): 500m development radius around functional railway stations in agricultural zones upon paying 30% ASR premium.
  • Konkan & Coastal Belts (Reg. 5.3 & 5.7): Sindhudurg tourism zones T-1 to T-5 (G+1 height cap); Raigad 1.0 km coastal wadi land safeguards; Sindhudurg gaothan expansion strictly capped at 200m.
  • Western Ghats & Mega-Science (Reg. 5.5, 5.6 & 5.9): Mahabaleshwar 2.5 km buffer; Satara lands above 1,000m MSL (Appendix O); Lonavala/Karla Sector-R min 500 sq.m plots with sloping roofs; Hingoli LIGO-India seismic quiet zone.
