STATUTORY HIGHLIGHTS

Key Chapter Takeaways

  • Mandatory Permission (Reg. 2.1): No development, construction, or material change of land use can commence without valid written permission / Commencement Certificate (CC) under Section 18 & 46 of MRTP Act.
  • 16 Statutory Exemptions (Reg. 2.1.2): Solar panels ≤ 1.8m, internal commercial partitions with structural stability cert, temporary site offices/sample flats, low-risk (≤ 150 sq.m) under Appendix K, and agro-tourism (≤ 8 rooms) under Appendix K-2.
  • Drawing Standards & Special Buildings (Reg. 2.2): Key plan (1:4000), site/layout (1:500/1000), building (1:100 with staircase section), Special Buildings ≥ 15m with 6.0m fire driveway, Table 2-A sizes, and Table 2-B coloring notations.
  • Fees & Levies (Reg. 2.2.12–2.2.14): Tiered scrutiny fees, Section 124 Development Charges on ASR rates, 50:50 premium sharing, and 8.5% p.a. interest installment payment options.
  • Strict Discretion Limits (Reg. 2.3–2.5): 50% dominant zone rule for split plots. Road Setbacks, FSI, and Parking can NEVER be relaxed under any circumstance!
  • Deemed Permission & Occupancy (Reg. 2.6–2.15): 60-day sanction deadline, deemed CC provisions (only if compliant!), 4-year CC validity with plinth perpetual milestone, Appendix F plinth checking, and 21-day Occupancy Certificate.
