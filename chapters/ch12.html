Chapter 12: Structural Safety, Water Supply, Drainage & Sanitary Requirements

Master the statutory engineering and public health foundations of Maharashtra UDCPR-2020: NBC Part-6 structural stability, Licensed Structural Engineer certification, anti-malaria interconnected borrow pits, accredited alternative material testing (with mandatory 2-year record retention), NBC Part-8 building services and the 1-floor vertical lift extension exemption, Table 12-A per capita water demands ($135\text{ lpcd}$ residential), Table 12-B dedicated flushing storage cisterns, crèche sanitation, institutional and public fixture schedules (Tables 12-C to 12-P), barrier-free accessible transit toilets ($1\text{ per }4,000$), and Regulation 12.7 commercial billboard prohibitions on heritage and government structures.

STATUTORY HIGHLIGHTS

Key Chapter Takeaways

  • NBC Part-6 Structural Mandatory Certification (Reg 12.1): Foundations, RCC, steel, and masonry must obey NBC Part-6 (Sections 1 to 7) and BIS seismic/fire codes. Applications for building permission require a statutory Certificate of Structural Stability signed by an empanelled Licensed Structural Engineer.
  • Anti-Malaria Borrow Pit Mandate (Reg 12.2.2): All borrow pits dug during construction must be deep, interconnected into continuous drainage trenches graded toward the lowest site contour, and discharged into a stream or storm sewer to eliminate standing water breeding mosquitoes.
  • Alternative Materials & 2-Year Record Retention (Reg 12.3): New materials (precast, 3D printing, composite panels) must demonstrate equivalence in strength, fire resistance, durability, and safety via advance testing by approved agencies at the owner's cost. The Authority must retain test records for at least two years.
  • Single-Floor Lift Extension Exemption (Reg 12.4.2): In existing buildings where one additional vertical floor is proposed, the existing elevator is statutorily exempt from being extended to the new top floor, saving societies massive structural retrofitting costs.
  • Water Supply Demands (Reg 12.5 & Table 12-A): Residential population is fixed at 5 persons per tenement consuming $135\text{ lpcd}$. Non-residential occupancies derive populations from Table 9-E occupant load factors. Key benchmarks: Hotel lodging $180\text{ lpcd}$ per bed, Day Schools $45\text{ lpcd}$, Hospitals $\le 100$ beds $340\text{ lpcd}$ / $> 100$ beds $450\text{ lpcd}$, Restaurants $70\text{ lpcd}$ per seat, and Cinemas $15\text{ lpcd}$ per seat.
  • Mandatory Dedicated Flushing Reserves (Table 12-B): Independent flushing cistern storage is compulsory: $270\text{ litres net}$ for the 1st WC + $180\text{ litres}$ for each additional WC in an apartment; $900\text{ litres per WC}$ in common tenements, factories, and assembly halls ($+350\text{ L per urinal}$).
  • Health Safeguards & Crèche Quotas (Reg 12.6.3): Drinking fountains are strictly banned inside toilets. Toxic/hazardous exposure areas must have an emergency deluge shower and eye-wash jet along an accessible wheelchair route. Workplaces with crèches must provide 1 WC per 10 persons, 1 washbasin per 15 persons, and a kitchen sink with drinking water tap.
  • Commercial & Transit Sanitation (Tables 12-C to 12-P): Cinemas use a statutory demographic ratio of 2/3 Male and 1/3 Female. Shopping malls mandate 1 WC per 50 floating patrons (min 2). Mass transit terminals (airports, railway stations) mandate at least 1 Accessible Toilet for the Disabled per 4,000 persons (Minimum 1).
  • Outdoor Advertisement Prohibitions (Reg 12.7): Commercial hoardings, sky-signs, and billboards are strictly prohibited on buildings of architectural, historical, or heritage importance, and on Government buildings (except signs for the government building's own official purpose).
