CHAPTER 13 • L9039 - L9425 // BARRIER-FREE, SOLAR, RWH, GREY WATER & DISASTER RESILIENCE

Chapter 13: Special Provisions for Certain Buildings

Master Maharashtra’s specialized architectural and environmental building controls: Barrier-Free Universal Design for Differently Abled persons (plots > 2,000 sq.m, 1,800 mm walkways, 3.6m parking bays ≤ 30m, 1:12 ramps, 13-passenger BIS lifts, outward-swinging accessible toilets), Rooftop Solar SWH/RTPV systems (plots > 4,000 sq.m, 25% roof area, 50 kg/sq.m load), Rain Water Harvesting with 4-layer percolation pits (plots ≥ 500 sq.m, 100% FSI free), Grey Water Recycling (≥ 100 flats, 5% tax rebate vs. water disconnection), on-site Solid Waste Composting (≥ 4,000 sq.m BUA), and the landmark 10th October 2024 Gazette Notification (Reg. 13.6) mandating 2-hour Fire Towers, intermediate 65m break tanks for $\ge$90m towers, and 5-year electrical safety audits.

STATUTORY HIGHLIGHTS

Key Chapter Takeaways

  • Universal Barrier-Free Design (Reg 13.1): Mandatory for public buildings on plots > 2,000 sq.m. Requires 1,800 mm walkways (max 5% slope), two 3.6-meter wide disabled parking spaces within 30.0 meters of entrance, 1:12 entrance ramps with 800 mm dual handrails extending 300 mm, 900 mm clear doors (max 12 mm threshold), 1,350 mm stairs (max 12 risers/flight), 13-passenger BIS wheelchair elevator (1,100 x 2,000 mm cage, 5-sec door hold, audio floor announcer), and accessible toilet cubicles (1,500 x 1,750 mm with mandatory outward-swinging door and 500 mm seat height).
  • Rooftop Solar SWH & RTPV (Reg 13.2): Compulsory on plots exceeding 4,000 sq.m. Developers must dedicate at least 25% of the terrace footprint, design roof slabs for an additional 50 kg/sq.m loading, install shadow-free open railings (>1 ft) on south/east/west parapets, and pre-lay insulated hot water distribution pipelines.
  • Rain Water Harvesting & 100% FSI Exemption (Reg 13.3): Mandatory on all layout open spaces/amenity spaces and plots ≥ 500 sq.m. Recharging permitted via open wells (≥1.0m dia × 6.0m depth), bore-wells (≥3.0m depth), or 4-layer percolation pits (50% 40mm metal, 20% 20mm metal, 20% coarse sand, top fine sand, with a 15 cm raised masonry curb). Requires min two 100mm φ down-takes per 100 sq.m roof area. All RWH structures are 100% exempt from FSI computation.
  • Grey Water Recycling & 5% Tax Rebate (Reg 13.4): Mandatory for group housing ≥ 100 tenements (150 for EWS/LIG), commercial/educational ≥ 1,500 sq.m BUA or 20,000 L/day demand, and hospitals with ≥ 40 beds. Earmarks dedicated plot in ≥10,000 sq.m layouts. Recycled water connects to dual plumbing for flushing and gardening. Active plants receive a 5% Property Tax rebate; defunct plants face daily fines of Rs. 300/- and immediate municipal water disconnection.
  • On-Site Wet Waste Composting (Reg 13.5): Mandatory for complexes ≥ 4,000 sq.m BUA and 3-star+ hotels: 100% of organic wet waste must be converted on-site via Organic Waste Composters (OWC) or vermiculture pits before municipal dry collection.
  • Landmark Disaster & Fire Safety Amendment (Reg 13.6 - Oct 10, 2024): Applies to buildings > 10,000 sq.m BUA or > 1,000 occupants. Mandates 2-hour Fire Towers with fireman evacuation lifts and ventilated lobbies, which statutorily exempts projects from providing duplicate second staircases and second lifts! In buildings ≥ 90 meters height, intermediate fire break water tanks and booster pumps are compulsory at every 65-meter vertical elevation stage. Enforces mandatory Licensed Electrical Engineer supervision, 5-year periodic inspections, and statutory Occupancy Certificate revocation for non-compliance.
