CHAPTER 7 • L5366 - L5966 // SPECIAL INCENTIVES & HIGHER FSI

Chapter 7: Higher FSI for Certain Uses

Unlock Maharashtra's highest-density urban development frameworks: Table 7-A institutional concessions (schools, hospitals, star hotels), road surrender FSI credit, MHADA colony redevelopment (3.00 gross FSI), 30-year-old private housing societies, Hyperscale Data Centers, and Central Business District commercial towers up to 5.00 FSI.

STATUTORY HIGHLIGHTS

Key Chapter Takeaways

  • Table 7-A Concessional Tariffs: Pre-primary/Primary schools pay 5% ASR premium; Charitable hospitals & colleges pay 10% ASR; Private institutions pay 15% ASR. Zero amenity space surrender under Reg. 7.0(h).
  • Starred Hotel One-Step Rule (Table 7-A): 2-star and above hotels pay 20% ASR premium and receive building potential calculated one road-width step below (e.g. 12m road receives 15m road potential).
  • Road Surrender FSI Credit (Reg. 7.2): Surrendering unencumbered land for DP roads or service roads generates 100% compensatory FSI credit for in-situ consumption or negotiable TDR script.
  • Staff Quarters FSI (Reg. 7.3): FSI of 2.50 to 3.00 on gross plot for PWD, MHADA, and Police Housing projects, with 100% exemption from premium, scrutiny fees, and Inclusive Housing. Up to 1/3rd permissible for free sale.
  • MHADA Colony Redevelopment (Reg. 7.4): Blanket 3.00 FSI on gross layout. Minimum 35 sq.m carpet guarantee (carpet + 35% + Table 7-B plot bonuses up to 45%). Basic Ratio ($LR/RC$) incentive sharing and 51% member consent eviction power.
  • 30-Year-Old Housing Societies (Reg. 7.6.1): Multi-dwelling societies > 30 years old receive 30% incentive FSI on existing BUA or 15 sq.m per flat, with a statutory minimum carpet area floor of 27.87 sq.m (300 sq.ft).
  • Tenanted Buildings (Reg. 7.6.2): Developers rehousing protected tenants receive 50% incentive FSI on the rehabilitation component based on 51% occupant consent.
  • IT & Hyperscale Data Centers (Reg. 7.8 - Jan 2024): FSI up to 4.00 on 27m roads at 10% ASR premium. Up to 40% support services (residential/commercial) and zero amenity space. 0.3%/day ASR penalty for unauthorized non-IT use.
  • CBD Commercial Towers (Reg. 7.13): FSI up to 5.00 on 27m roads at 50% ASR premium. Residential use strictly capped at 30% of Table 6-G potential.
  • Green Building Bonuses (Reg. 7.10): +3%, +5%, or +7% incentive FSI on basic FSI for GRIHA/IGBC/LEED/GEM certified green buildings.
