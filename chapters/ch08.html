STATUTORY HIGHLIGHTS

Key Chapter Takeaways

  • Permissible Parking Locations (Reg 8.1.1(i)): Basements, stilts, podium decks, upper MLCP floors, surface yards, and lock-up garages. Stilt clear headroom must measure $\ge 2.40\text{ m}$ from the bottom (soffit) of beams; stack parking allows up to $4.50\text{ m}$.
  • Table 8-A Stall Dimensions & Concessions: Standard car stall is $2.50\text{ m} \times 5.00\text{ m}$. Under Note (1)(a), up to 50% may be compact stalls of $2.30\text{ m} \times 4.50\text{ m}$. Mechanized puzzle trays mandate $2.3\text{ m} \times 5.8\text{ m}$ (large cars) and $2.1\text{ m} \times 5.0\text{ m}$ (small cars). Scooters require $1.00\text{ m} \times 2.00\text{ m}$.
  • Driveway & Circulation Clearances (Reg 8.1.1(iv)): Driveways must be completely exclusive of stall footprints: minimum $3.00\text{ m}$ clear width for cars and $2.00\text{ m}$ for scooters.
  • Loading / Unloading Berths (Reg 8.1.1(viii)(b)): Commercial, office, and industrial complexes require 1 berth ($3.75\text{ m} \times 7.50\text{ m}$) per $1,000\text{ sq.m}$ carpet exceeding the first $200\text{ sq.m}$. Statutory ceiling: Maximum 4 berths for offices and Maximum 6 berths for other buildings.
  • Bus Bay Mandate (Reg 8.1.1(vi)): Compulsory for schools, multiplexes, malls, assembly buildings, and group housing schemes exceeding 500 flats.
  • Marginal Open Space Parking & Fire Driveways (Reg 8.1.1(viii)(d)): Surface parking in marginal spaces is allowed only if an unobstructed $3.0\text{ m}$ ($6.0\text{ m}$ for Special Buildings) perimeter fire driveway is preserved 100% clear around the building.
  • Mechanical Parking Towers in Margins (Reg 8.1.1(viii)(f)): Permitted at $1.50\text{ m}$ setback from side/rear boundaries provided a $6.0\text{ m}$ driveway, $9.0\text{ m}$ fire turning circle, 2-hour fire-rated dead wall, and NBC-2016 Part 4 Table 7 fire systems are provided.
  • Table 8-B Residential Tenement Tiers: Calibrated per unit carpet area: $\ge 150\text{ sq.m}$ ($2\text{ cars} + 1\text{ scooter}$), $80-150\text{ sq.m}$ ($1\text{ car} + 1\text{ scooter}$), $40-80\text{ sq.m}$ ($1\text{ car} + 2\text{ scooters}$ per 2 units), plus mandatory 5% visitor parking.
  • The Two-Wheeler Non-Reduction Shield (Note vii - 2022/2023): Table 8-C discount factors DO NOT apply to two-wheeler parking for Multi-Family Residential. 100% of residential scooter parking is mandatory across all cities and rural areas!
  • Excess Parking Surcharge & Public Surrender (Note v - Jan 2024): Parking exceeding 150% of requirement incurs a 10% Land ASR surcharge on excess stall area. Unphased surplus parking built in Phase 1 must be surrendered as free public parking if balance proposals are not submitted prior to final OC.
