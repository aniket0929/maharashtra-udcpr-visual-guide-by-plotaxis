Chapter 11: Acquisition of Reserved Sites & Transferable Development Rights

Master Maharashtra’s statutory land acquisition and development rights framework: the Accommodation Reservation Principle (Table 11-A), Transferable Development Rights (TDR) generation multipliers ($2.0\times$ non-congested, $3.0\times$ congested, $1.35\times$ construction formula), the universal ASR ratio indexation formula ($X = \frac{R_g}{R_r} \times Y$), road-width receiving caps, and Reservation Credit Certificates (RCC) with 10% holding discounts.

STATUTORY HIGHLIGHTS

Key Chapter Takeaways

  • Accommodation Reservation (Reg 11.1 & Table 11-A): Private owners develop DP reservations by surrendering an independent plot and turn-key constructed amenity to the Authority free of cost ($40\%$ land / $50\%$ built in A/B/C Class Corporations; $35\% / 25\%$ in D Class; $30\% / 20\%$ in Councils).
  • 100% Gross FSI Transfer (Note xi): The owner retains the remaining $60\%$ parcel and is entitled to develop it with the full FSI and TDR potential of the entire original gross plot without any in-situ capping!
  • Deemed Rezoning & Composite Relief (Notes iv & xiii): The retained parcel automatically converts into a Residential or Commercial zone upon final OC. Small plots can build composite buildings by surrendering ground/stilt floors and paying a $40\%$ ASR land premium.
  • TDR Land Surrender Multipliers (Reg 11.2.4): Surrender of unencumbered reserved land generates $2.00\times$ the land area in non-congested zones and $3.00\times$ in congested zones ($1.85\times$ and $2.85\times$ if the $1.5\text{ m}$ compound wall is omitted). DP roads receive full $2.00\times / 3.00\times$ credit without compound walls.
  • Construction Amenity TDR Formula (Reg 11.2.5): Constructing the public amenity or DP road yields bonus TDR: $\text{Amenity TDR} = \left(\frac{A}{B}\right) \times 1.35$, factoring comprehensive PWD DSR construction costs ($A$) against the local ASR land rate ($B$) with a $1.35$ incentive multiplier.
  • The Universal ASR Indexation Formula (Reg 11.2.6): When TDR travels between zones, it is equalized by land value: $X = \left(\frac{R_g}{R_r}\right) \times Y$, where both generating ($R_g$) and receiving ($R_r$) rates are frozen at the historic generating year of the DRC.
  • Zero Infrastructure Improvement Charges (Reg 11.2.11): Municipal corporations are strictly barred from levying betterment or infrastructure charges on TDR utilization.
  • Reservation Credit Certificate (RCC) (Reg 11.3): Rupee-denominated, non-interest bearing, transferable financial credit issued in lieu of immediately required land. Directly offsets Development Charges, Premium FSI, and annual Property Taxes, with a $10\%$ statutory discount on redemptions made after $6\text{ months}$.
