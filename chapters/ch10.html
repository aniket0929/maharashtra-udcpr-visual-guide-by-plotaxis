Chapter 10: City Specific Regulations

Master local statutory overrides, historical royal sanads, and municipal planning variations across Maharashtra: Pune (PMC high-rises & Koregaon Park), Thane (commercial spines, Metro PPL & hazard buffers), Nagpur (NMC commercial FSI & NMRDA Ring Road), Nashik & Kolhapur (Mahalaxmi Kiranotsav Marg), Navi Mumbai (NMMC & CIDCO 10.10.2 mega-redevelopment), and MMR logistics hubs (Bhiwandi BSNA, Ulhasnagar, & Panvel).

STATUTORY HIGHLIGHTS

Key Chapter Takeaways

  • Pune Municipal Corporation (Reg 10.1): High-rise road width thresholds ($12\text{ m}$ road for height $>36\text{ m}$; $15\text{ m}$ road for height $\ge 50\text{ m}$); Koregaon Park Collector's Sanad Rules (max $1/3$ coverage, G+1 storeys, 20 ft front/rear margins, zero subdivision); Parvati & Chatushrungi $21.0\text{ m}$ view caps; ARDE Pashan ($75\text{ m}$) & HEMRL Sutarwadi ($457.2\text{ m}$) defense buffers.
  • Thane Commercial Corridors & Metro PPL (Reg 10.2): Stepped front setbacks on Ram Maruti ($3.05\text{ m}$ GF / $4.5\text{ m}$ upper) and Gokhale Road ($2.30\text{ m}$ GF / $4.5\text{ m}$ upper); Eastern Express Highway $7.5\text{ m}$ non-buildable setback; Metro Public Parking Lots (PPL) with 50% Incentive FSI; TMC dilapidated building renewal ($70\text{ m}$ height on $9\text{--}12\text{ m}$ roads, $1.5\text{ m}$ podium margins, and $0.80$ parking factor).
  • Thane Hazard Concentric Rings & Defense (Reg 10.2.4--8): Chemical industry $100\text{ m}$ Green Belt (repairs only; acquired via TDR) plus $150\text{ m}$ Low Density Zone (FSI $0.50$, max G+2) with automatic suo-moto cessation upon factory relocation; Air Force Station $100\text{ m}$ restricted zone (no digging, no trees); Kolshet/Kavesar naval radar height plates; Yeur Village G-1 & G-3 forest zones.
  • Nagpur Core & Metro Region (Reg 10.3 & 10.4): NMC Commercial Zone base FSI $2.50$ (pure commercial) and $2.00$ (mixed); Industrial base FSI $2.50$; NIT leased I-to-R conversions ($20\%$ ASR comm / $15\%$ mix); NMRDA $250\text{ m}$ residential ribbon along $60\text{ m}$ Outer Ring Road; $15\text{-Hectare}$ Agricultural townships with $10\%$ free land surrender; Gaothan peripheral expansion ($750\text{ m}$ / $1,000\text{ m}$).
  • Nashik & Kolhapur Heritage Enclaves (Reg 10.5 & 10.9): Dedicated Godavari River & nallah cycle tracks; Gangapur--Ambad $36\text{ m}$ DP road relaxed $3.00\text{ m}$ front setback plate; Kolhapur Table 10-B historic layouts (Rajarampuri, Shahupuri, Laxmipuri, Tarabai Park); and the sacred Mahalaxmi Temple Kiranotsav Marg solar sun-ray height caps fixed by the Municipal Commissioner.
  • Navi Mumbai & CIDCO Mega-Renewal (Reg 10.10 & 10.14): Base FSI $1.50$ on plots $\ge 1,000\text{ m}^2$ with road $\ge 15\text{ m}$ ($+0.50$ bonus over Table 6-G); Regulation 10.10.2 redevelopment for CIDCO buildings $>30$ years old (FSI up to $3.00$, existing carpet $+ 35\%$, min $300\text{ sq.ft}$, and Table 10-D cluster bonuses up to $20\%$); $12.5\%$ Land Compensation Schemes (GES) and Pushpak Node TDR.
  • MMR Growth Centers & Logistics (Reg 10.6--16): Vasai-Virar Low Density Zone ($0.50$ max FSI, zero TDR); Ulhasnagar Regularisation Act ($4.00\text{ FSI} + \text{Ancillary}$, min $4,000\text{ m}^2$ URS); Bhiwandi BSNA $3.00\text{ FSI}$ Affordable Housing Scheme with 4-stage release; Panvel PMC $75\%$ TDR conversion at $60\%$ ASR premium; and transitional sunset clauses.
