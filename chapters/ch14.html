Chapter 14: Special Schemes

Master Maharashtra’s flagship large-scale urban development schemes: Integrated Township Projects (ITP, 40 Ha, 2.00 FSI, 20% social housing), Transit Oriented Development (TOD, 500m Metro buffers, FSI up to 4.00, 50% parking cuts, 50:50 premium split with Metro SPVs), Affordable Housing Schemes (AHS, 4,000 sqm, 1:3 land partition, 27.88 sqm unit caps) & PMAY (up to 2.50 basic FSI without premium/TDR), Heritage Conservation & Heritage TDR (Grades I/II/III, HCC oversight, skyline preservation), Slum Rehabilitation Schemes (SRS, 51% consent, 27.88 sqm free homes, 1:R incentive formulas, +30% density bonuses, Oct-2024 staircase caps, Slum TDR), Urban Renewal Schemes (URS Cluster Redevelopment, 10,000 sqm non-congested vs 4,000 sqm congested, min 30 sqm + 25% bonus for legal owners, FSI 4.00+), and Special Industrial Ecosystems (Eco-tourism buffers, Integrated IT Townships IITP, Logistics Parks ILP with +200% FSI, Aerospace/Defense, and MIDC IIA).

STATUTORY HIGHLIGHTS

Key Chapter Takeaways

  • Integrated Township Projects (Reg 14.1): Private smart cities requiring minimum 40 Hectares (100 Acres) contiguous land on an 18.0m road. Enforces a strict 60% Residential, 10% Commercial, 10% Parks, 15% Roads land use balance. Unlocks 2.00 FSI on gross land, 50% stamp duty exemption, and deemed NA conversion in return for Tables 14-A to 14-J amenities (50-bed hospital, fire station, police station) and handing over 20% residential BUA for EWS/LIG social housing.
  • Transit Oriented Development (Reg 14.2): Statutorily active within 500 meters of Metro station boundaries (and 100m of PCMC BRT). FSI scales by road width from 2.50 (9m road) up to 4.00 (24m road). Premium (30% to 35% of ASR) is split 50:50 between Planning Authority and Metro SPV. Mandates 1/4th TDR pairing, requires 50% of residential FSI to be in ≤60 sqm flats, slashes statutory parking by 50%, and offers FSI-free bonuses for public parking within 200 meters.
  • Affordable Housing & PMAY (Reg 14.3 & 14.4): Under AHS, plots ≥ 4,000 sq.m on 18m roads get 3.00 FSI, partitioned 1:3 between Affordable (25% land) and Free Sale (75% land). Affordable flats are exactly 27.88 sq.m. carpet area handed over free to ULBs via Table 14-S milestones, paying off-site infra charges (5% ASR, min ₹2,000/sqm). Under PMAY, up to 2.50 FSI is basic allowable FSI with ZERO premium and ZERO TDR in developable zones, with 1.00 FSI in peripheral green zones.
  • Heritage Conservation & Heritage TDR (Reg 14.5): Establishes statutory listing (Appendix-L card) and 3-tier grading: Grade I (careful preservation; like-to-like repairs; zero high-rises), Grade II (intelligent conservation; adaptive reuse; harmonious wings), and Grade III (townscape character). Regulated by multi-disciplinary Heritage Conservation Committees (HCC). Owners deprived of utilizing permissible FSI are compensated via Heritage TDR (DRC). Bans commercial billboards and preserves skylines.
  • Slum Rehabilitation Schemes (Reg 14.6 & 14.7): 51% registered dweller consent unlocks free 27.88 sq.m. (300 sq.ft.) carpet ownership flats for protected occupiers (cut-off: 01-Jan-2000). Commercial viability is powered by statutory incentive formula 1 : R = [2.8 - 0.3n] (min 1.50, max 3.00) based on ASR flat and construction rates. Offers +20% to +30% high-density bonuses and +10% cluster bonuses. Oct-2024 Gazette caps common areas at 60% of rehab BUA. Unconsumed FSI generates Slum TDR: X = (Rg / Rr) × Y.
  • Urban Renewal Schemes / Cluster Redevelopment (Reg 14.8): Revitalizes decaying cores over minimum 10,000 sq.m. (non-congested) or 4,000 sq.m. (congested gaothan) clusters on 18m roads. Covers buildings >30 years old, dilapidated structures, and up to 25% slums. Legal owners receive old carpet (min 30 sq.m.) PLUS a mandatory 25% bonus carpet free of cost (Table 14-X). Total FSI is Rehab + Incentive, or 4.00 FSI whichever is higher, based on Land Rate to Construction Rate (LR/RC) ratios. Unconsumed FSI yields Urban Renewal TDR (URT).
  • Special Industrial & Logistics Ecosystems (Reg 14.9 to 14.13): Reg 14.9 Eco-Tourism permits 10% BUA (9m G+1) within 5km of sanctuaries with open chainlink fencing and 50% solar power. Reg 14.10 IITP (Jan 2024 amendment) enables 10-acre IT campuses with 2.50 FSI, 50:50 IT/support split, and 140 lpcd water. Reg 14.11 Logistics Parks (ILP, min 5 acres on 15m roads) unlocks up to 200% additional FSI at 0-15% premium with 24.0m warehouse heights. Reg 14.12 grants +0.50 FSI bonuses for Aerospace & Defense R&D facilities.

Reg. 14.4.1 (#) Clarified Chapter 14: Special Schemes
📅 Order No. CR 42/21/UD 12 dt. 14th June, 2021 (Item 13)

PMAY Affordable Housing: Basic FSI vs Road Potential Stacking

❓ The Practical Ambiguity / Dispute

Prior to UDCPR, PMAY schemes received a flat 2.50 FSI on roads 15m or wider. Under UDCPR 14.4.1 Condition 3, permissible FSI is capped at maximum building potential under Reg 6.1 or 6.3 subject to max 2.5 treated as allowable basic FSI without premium or TDR. Planners were confused about road width thresholds and whether potential beyond 2.5 could be loaded.

⚖️ Statutory Govt Ruling (Sec. 154 MRTP)

The Government clarified that: (1) In congested areas (Table 6-A), max 2.50 FSI is permissible on minimum 9.0m roads; (2) In non-congested areas (Table 6-G), max 2.50 FSI is permissible on minimum 15.0m roads. Any residual building potential above 2.50 permitted by road width can still be availed by paying Premium FSI or loading TDR.

📐 Practical Takeaway for Architects & Students

PMAY projects enjoy 2.50 free basic FSI without paying premium or TDR charges on >=9m roads in gaothan or >=15m outside gaothan. If the road allows higher potential (e.g. 3.00 FSI on 24m road), the top 0.50 FSI can be unlocked via standard premium/TDR.

Reg. 14.8.7 (#) Clarified Chapter 14: Special Schemes
📅 Order No. CR 42/21/UD 12 dt. 14th June, 2021 (Item 17)

DP Reservations Falling inside Urban Renewal Clusters (URC)

❓ The Practical Ambiguity / Dispute

Under Regulation 14.8.7, an Implementing Agency must construct public amenities designated on the Development Plan (e.g. municipal schools, health centers, parking lots) and hand them over free of cost and free of FSI to the Municipal Corporation. Practitioners and developers were deadlocked: Does the agency receive Construction Amenity TDR to recover construction costs, or must they bear 100% of the expenditure without compensation?

⚖️ Statutory Govt Ruling (Sec. 154 MRTP)

The Urban Development Department ruled that under Regulation 14.8.14(xvi), any development aspect not expressly barred under URS is governed by general UDCPR provisions. Consequently, for constructing and handing over the built-up amenity area to the Corporation, the Implementing Agency IS STATUTORILY ENTITLED to Construction Amenity TDR under Regulation 11.2.5.

📐 Practical Takeaway for Architects & Students

Cluster redevelopment feasibility is protected: building required DP public amenities inside a cluster scheme does not drain capital, as the built area is compensated via market-saleable Construction Amenity TDR under Chapter 11.
