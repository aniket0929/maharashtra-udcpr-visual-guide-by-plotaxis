Chapter 3: General Land Development Requirements

Master the spatial rules for land layout, subdivision, and group housing: site clearance buffers from rivers and high-tension lines, mandatory internal roads, 10% Recreational Open Space, Amenity Space surrender, and 20% Inclusive Housing.

STATUTORY HIGHLIGHTS

Key Chapter Takeaways

  • Site Clearances (Reg. 3.1): Strict safety buffers from water bodies (blue line / red line), electric transmission lines (horizontal/vertical clearance), railway tracks, and defence boundaries.
  • Internal Roads (Reg. 3.3): Road width dictated by length (up to 75m: 6m/9m, up to 150m: 9m, up to 300m: 12m, > 300m: 15m).
  • Recreational Open Space (Reg. 3.4): Mandatory 10% open green space on plots ≥ 0.40 ha (4,000 sq.m). Permitted 10% clubhouse construction.
  • Amenity Space (Reg. 3.5): 1% to 5% statutory provision based on authority category and land size.
  • Inclusive Housing (Reg. 3.8): Mandatory 20% affordable housing (EWS/LIG) quota on gross plots ≥ 4,000 sq.m.
  • Net Plot Area (Reg. 3.9): Formula for deducting DP road widening, public reservations, and surrendered amenity spaces.
