Chapter 15: Regulations for Special Activities / Plans

Master Maharashtra’s specialized operational planning controls: Quarrying & Mining Operations in Agricultural zones (Reg 15.1, 200m/500m blasting buffers, Director of Geology & Mining approvals, 0.50% ASR development charges, and 3-year validation cycles), Telecommunication Cell Sites & Mobile Towers (Reg 15.2 per DoT guidelines), Preparation of Local Area Plans (Reg 15.3 micro-planning under MRTP Section 33), and Comprehensive Street Design Guidelines for inclusive, pedestrian-first urban corridors (Reg 15.4).

STATUTORY HIGHLIGHTS

Key Chapter Takeaways

  • Quarrying & Mining Controls (Reg 15.1): Permitted exclusively in Agricultural Zones outside CRZ, heritage precincts, and eco-sensitive zones. Requires 1:5,000 location plan, 1:500 contour excavation plan, and restoration plan approved by the Director of Geology & Mining. Mandates a 200-meter buffer from 30m roads, railway lines, and settlements (increased to 500 meters for blasting operations). Development charge is 0.50% of developed land ASR. Permissions valid for 1 year, renewable up to 3 years max.
  • Telecommunication & Mobile Towers (Reg 15.2): Erection of cell sites and rooftop telecom towers governed by Central DoT and State IT Department guidelines. Integrated with the 25-Aug-2023 directives u/s 154 under Model Building Bye-Laws 2016 for 5G small cells on street furniture and mandatory In-Building Solutions (IBS).
  • Local Area Plans - LAP (Reg 15.3): Authorizes Planning Authorities to prepare micro-level neighborhood masterplans overriding general Development Plans. Follows procedure under Section 33 of the MRTP Act 1966; once sanctioned by the State Government, provisions of the Local Area Plan prevail over the UDCPR.
  • Universal Street Design Standards (Reg 15.4): Mandates complete street design for all urban roads, ensuring segregated pedestrian sidewalks, dedicated cycle tracks, multi-utility zones, street furniture, tree canopies, and unhampered barrier-free access per Appendix-M specimen drawings (18m to 60m).

📅 Order No. CR 42/21/UD 12 dt. 14th June, 2021 (Item 14)

Uniform Statewide Licensing Fees for Technical Personnel (BPMS)

❓ The Practical Ambiguity / Dispute

Several Municipal Corporations (e.g. PMC, PCMC, TMC) had passed local general body resolutions charging exorbitant annual licensing fees from architects and structural engineers, far exceeding UDCPR rates.

⚖️ Statutory Govt Ruling (Sec. 154 MRTP)

The Government directed that with statewide implementation of the online BPMS (Building Plan Management System), uniform standards must prevail. Local authorities are strictly prohibited from charging licensing fees higher than the statutory fees in Reg C-7.2 (Rs. 3,000 for 3 years for Architects/Engineers/Planners; Rs. 1,500 for Supervisors).

📐 Practical Takeaway for Architects & Students

Architects licensed with the Town Planning Department are licensed across Maharashtra. Municipalities cannot charge arbitrary local registration levies beyond UDCPR Appendix C.
