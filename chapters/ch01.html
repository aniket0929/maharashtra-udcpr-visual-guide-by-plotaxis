Chapter 1: Administration & Definitions

The foundational legal authority of UDCPR-2020. Understand where these regulations apply across Maharashtra, what is excluded, and the 141 core terms defined under Regulation 1.3.

STATUTORY HIGHLIGHTS

Key Chapter Takeaways

  • Applicability (Reg. 1.1): Applies to all Planning Authorities and Regional Plan areas in Maharashtra State except Municipal Corporation of Greater Mumbai (MCGM), MIDC, NAINA, JNPT, Hill Station Councils, and Eco-sensitive zones.
  • Definitions (Reg. 1.3): Exactly 141 statutory definitions (Act, FSI, Carpet Area, TDR, DRC, High-Rise, Amenity Space, etc.).
  • Savings (Reg. 1.5): Any development permission or layout approved before December 2, 2020 remains valid, but any revision may opt for UDCPR in its entirety.
  • Removal of Difficulties (Reg. 1.10): Empowers the State Government to issue Corrigenda, Addenda, and executive clarifications.
