Chapter 4: Land Use Classification & Permissible Uses

Master the complete zoning framework of Maharashtra: Pure Residential (R-1) vs Mixed-Use (R-2), commercial and manufacturing zones, the statutory 23.0m industrial buffer, I-to-R conversion masterclass, agricultural non-agri permissions, and civic reservations.

STATUTORY HIGHLIGHTS

Key Chapter Takeaways

  • R-1 vs. R-2 (Reg. 4.3 & 4.4): Governed automatically by abutting road width (< 12m = R-1; ≥ 12m = R-2 outside gaothan; 9.0m in congested/C-class). Convenience shopping allowed only on ground floor in R-1.
  • Home Occupations & Professional Offices (Reg. 4.3): Customary family occupations allowed up to 1 H.P.; professional offices in own tenements allowed up to 50 sq.m carpet area.
  • Industrial Buffer (Reg. 4.8): Mandatory 23.0m separation setback to residential zones (includes road width), 100% credited toward FSI. Up to 25% BUA allowed for worker quarters.
  • I-to-R Conversion (Reg. 4.8.1): Converting industrial plots to residential requires 5% ASR premium, Labour Commissioner NOC for closed plants, and 10%/15% tiered amenity surrender with full compensatory FSI credit.
  • Agricultural Zone Non-Agri Uses (Reg. 4.11): Farmhouses strictly capped at 0.40 Ha min, 0.04 FSI, and 400 sq.m max (G+1, 9m ht). 1.00 FSI for educational/medical institutions; solar panel ground coverage exempt from FSI.
  • Water & Slope Protection (Reg. 4.12 & 4.17): Mandatory 15.0m river buffer / 9.0m nallah buffer. Slopes steeper than 1:5 gradient are non-buildable HTHS.
  • Civic Reservations & P/SP (Reg. 4.10 & 4.27): 15% basic FSI permitted for ancillary commercial use in institutional campuses; playground pavilions capped at 15% ground coverage.

Reg. 4.8.1(xvi) (#) Clarified Chapter 4: Permissible Uses Across Zones
📅 Order No. CR 72/23/UD-12 dt. 25th August, 2023

Inclusive Housing in Industrial-to-Residential Conversions

❓ The Practical Ambiguity / Dispute

When converting obsolete industrial land to residential or commercial use, developers asked whether standard 20% Inclusive Housing under Regulation 3.8 applies as land surrender or tenement quota.

⚖️ Statutory Govt Ruling (Sec. 154 MRTP)

The Government clarified that regular Regulation 3.8 surrender does not apply directly. Instead, 20% of the land or FSI proposed for residential use must be earmarked for small plots (<100 sq.m.) in plotted layouts or affordable tenements (<50 sq.m. built-up) in group housing.

📐 Practical Takeaway for Architects & Students

Industrial zone conversions carry an inescapable affordable housing mandate: allocate at least 20% of residential potential to units under 50 sq.m.

Reg. 4.11(ix) (#) Clarified Chapter 4: Permissible Uses Across Zones
📅 Order No. CR 42/21/UD 12 dt. 14th June, 2021 (Item 7)

Farm Houses in Agricultural Zones: Separate Accessory Structures

❓ The Practical Ambiguity / Dispute

Regulation 4.11(ix) allows one farmhouse per holding with FSI 0.04 up to max 400 sqm. Authorities insisted that all built area—including cattle sheds, tractor/implement garages, and fertilizer stores—must be joined under one single roof/building.

⚖️ Statutory Govt Ruling (Sec. 154 MRTP)

The Government clarified that farming operations require separating human dwelling from cattle barns, agro-equipment, and crop storage. The allowable 400 sqm built-up area does NOT need to be in a single building; the residential farmhouse and separate accessory agricultural buildings are permissible across the plot holding.

📐 Practical Takeaway for Architects & Students

You can design a separate farmhouse residence and detached farm shed/barn, provided total aggregate built-up area remains within 400 sq.m. and FSI 0.04.
