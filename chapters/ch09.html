STATUTORY HIGHLIGHTS

Key Chapter Takeaways

  • Plinth Elevation & Flood Protections (Reg 9.1): Minimum $30\text{ cm}$ plinth above ground level; elevated to $\ge 45\text{ cm}$ above High Flood Level (HFL) in flood zones; $15\text{ cm}$ for covered garages.
  • Habitable Room Heights (Table 9-A): Standard flat-roof rooms require $\ge 2.75\text{ m}$ ($2.40\text{ m}$ if AC; beam soffit minimum $\ge 2.40\text{ m}$). Commercial shops, offices, and assembly halls require $\ge 3.00\text{ m}$ (up to $6.00\text{ m}+$ without FSI penalty).
  • Sanitary Minimums & Heights (Reg 9.4): Bath: $1.00\text{m} \times 1.20\text{m}$; WC: $0.90\text{m} \times 0.90\text{m}$; Combined: $1.50\text{ sq.m}$ (min width $1.00\text{ m}$). Clear height $\ge 2.10\text{ m}$. Window $\ge 0.30\text{ sq.m}$.
  • Lofts vs Mezzanines (Reg 9.5 & 9.7): Lofts ($2.10\text{ m}$ headroom below, max $1.50\text{ m}$ height) allow 25% coverage in habitable rooms and 100% over toilets (free of FSI). Mezzanine floors are capped at 50% of room carpet area, mandate $2.10\text{ m}$ headroom below, must be set back $1.80\text{ m}$ from front wall, and are strictly counted in FSI.
  • Basement Free vs Counted FSI (Reg 9.11): Parking, AC machinery, DG sets, substations, ETPs, and Data Center storage are 100% Free of FSI. First-basement mall retail, bank vaults, mortuaries, and tenant storage are Counted in FSI. Ground-flushed basements can extend to within $1.50\text{ m}$ of plot boundaries.
  • Vehicular Ramps & Car Lifts (Reg 9.12): Maximum vehicular slope is 1 in 8. Requires two $3.00\text{ m}$ ramps or one $6.00\text{ m}$ ramp. Plots $\le 1,000\text{ sq.m}$ or $\le 2,000\text{ sq.m}$ may deploy 2 Car Lifts in lieu of ramps.
  • Table 9-C Ventilation Shafts: Toilet shafts scale with building height: $1.20\text{ sq.m}$ ($\le 10\text{m}$) up to $9.00\text{ sq.m}$ with min dimension $3.00\text{ m}$ for towers $>30\text{m}$ (where mechanical exhaust blowers are compulsory).
  • Table 9-D Travel Distances & Sprinklers: Maximum travel distance is $22.50\text{ m}$ for residential/schools and $30.00\text{ m}$ for commercial. +50% bonus for 100% sprinklered buildings ($33.75\text{ m}$ and $45.00\text{ m}$).
  • Refuge Areas Above 24.0 Metres (Reg 9.29.6): Compulsory for towers $>24\text{ m}$. Minimum $15\text{ sq.m}$ or $0.3\text{ sq.m/person}$ for 2 consecutive floors. Stacking: 1st above $24\text{m}$, 2nd above $39\text{m}$, and every $15\text{m}$ thereafter (100% free of FSI).
  • Mega-Towers Above 70.0 Metres (Reg 9.29.9): Compulsory 4-hour fire chute shafts ($2.5\text{m} \times 1.5\text{m}$) or external Fire Evacuation Towers with a pressurized smoke lobby raised 75 mm step-up to prevent firefighting water ingress.

Reg. 9.13 (#) Clarified Chapter 9: General Building Requirements - Parts of Building
📅 Order No. CR 42/21/UD 12 dt. 14th June, 2021 (Item 10)

Podium Setbacks for Special Buildings vs Non-Special Buildings

❓ The Practical Ambiguity / Dispute

Regulation 9.13(ii) requires a 6m distance from plot boundaries for podiua in Special Buildings. Clarification was sought on whether the front margin must also be 6m or if Table 6-D applies.

⚖️ Statutory Govt Ruling (Sec. 154 MRTP)

The Government clarified that for Special Buildings, the front setback for a parking podium is governed by Table 6-D front margin, while side and rear boundaries must leave minimum 6.0m clear for fire tenders. For Non-Special Buildings, regular marginal setbacks under the regulations apply.

📐 Practical Takeaway for Architects & Students

Podiums can align with the Table 6-D front building line, but must step back at least 6.00m on all side and rear edges for high-rise fire circulation.

Reg. 9.28.8 (#) Clarified Chapter 9: General Building Requirements - Parts of Building
📅 Order No. CR 42/21/UD 12 dt. 14th June, 2021 (Item 12)

1.20m Staircase Width Relief for Existing Buildings Crossing 24m

❓ The Practical Ambiguity / Dispute

When an existing sanctioned building with a 1.20m staircase sought vertical expansion using newly available UDCPR FSI, the height exceeded 24m, triggering a requirement for a 1.50m wide staircase under Table 9-G. Relocating existing load-bearing RCC columns was physically impossible.

⚖️ Statutory Govt Ruling (Sec. 154 MRTP)

The Government provided hardship relief: If floor-wise gross built area is under 750 sq.m. and occupant load calculations support egress, the existing 1.20m staircase is permissible above 24m, PROVIDED an independent external fire escape staircase is provided.

📐 Practical Takeaway for Architects & Students

Brownfield vertical extensions above 24m are not killed by RCC staircase columns if floor plates are <=750 sqm and an external fire staircase is installed.

Reg. 9.27.1 & 9.29.8 (#) Clarified Chapter 9: General Building Requirements - Parts of Building
📅 Order No. CR 42/21/UD 12 dt. 14th June, 2021 (Item 11)

Fire Lift Requirement for Buildings Between 15m and 24m Height

❓ The Practical Ambiguity / Dispute

For mid-rise buildings between 15m and 24m height, scrutiny officers insisted on two separate lifts (one passenger lift AND one dedicated fire lift), increasing core costs and structural load for smaller developments.

⚖️ Statutory Govt Ruling (Sec. 154 MRTP)

The Government clarified that for buildings between 15.0m and 24.0m height, providing ONE fire lift is mandatory. An additional passenger lift is NOT required by law for this height category, provided Fire CFO NOC is secured.

📐 Practical Takeaway for Architects & Students

Mid-rise buildings (15m to 24m) can operate with a single high-spec Fire Lift serving daily passenger traffic as well as emergency evacuation.
