High-Rise Fire Safety, Evacuation Stairs & Refuge Floors

Master Maharashtra's high-rise statutory fire regulations: the critical 15m, 24m, 50m, and 70m+ height thresholds, refuge floor design rules (every 7th floor above 24m), dual pressurized escape staircases (min 1.5m / 2.0m width), and Chief Fire Officer (CFO) NOC prerequisites.

01 // STATUTORY STEPS

The 4 Critical Statutory Height Thresholds

Step 1 • H > 15.0m
Special Building Trigger

CFO Fire NOC becomes mandatory. Wet riser system, yard hydrants, and minimum 4.5m marginal distances required (Reg. 1.3(113)).

Step 2 • H > 24.0m
Statutory High-Rise & Refuge

Classified as High-Rise. Mandatory 6.0m all-around fire driveway. First Refuge Floor must be provided immediately above 24.0m.

Step 3 • H > 50.0m
High-Rise Committee Scrutiny

Special High-Rise Building Committee sanction. Two separate pressurized fire escape staircases (min 1.5m / 2.0m width) with air-locks.

Step 4 • H > 70.0m
Super High-Rise Automation

Fire evacuation lifts, automated voice alarm systems, 200,000L static water storage, and helipad feasibility review on terrace.

03 // STATUTORY SPECIFICATION

Refuge Floor Design Rules (Regulation 9.29.2)

  • Mandatory Trigger Height: The first refuge floor must be located immediately above 24.0 meters from ground plinth. Thereafter, successive refuge floors must be provided at every 7th habitable floor.
  • Area Sizing Equation: Calculated at 0.30 sq.m per person for the entire population of the 7 floors it serves, subject to an absolute minimum of 15.00 sq.m.
  • 100% FSI Exemption: Under Regulation 6.3.3, approved refuge areas are completely exempt from FSI computation and do not attract premium charges.
  • Fire Door & Egress: The door leading to the refuge area must have a 2-hour fire resistance rating, open outwards into the refuge area, and be equipped with automatic panic hardware.
  • Prohibition of Commercial Exploitation: Refuge areas cannot be enclosed with glass, rented as clubhouses, or used for storage. Doing so constitutes a criminal safety violation under the Maharashtra Fire Prevention Act.
04 // SCRUTINY WATCHOUTS

Top 5 High-Rise Plan Scrutiny Pitfalls

  • 1. Providing a Single Staircase for Buildings > 15.0m: Reg. 9.28 and CFO mandates strictly require at least two independent, enclosed fire escape staircases for all buildings exceeding 15m in height (or exceeding 500 sqm per floor plate).
  • 2. Omitting Positive Pressure Ventilation in Escape Staircases: For buildings exceeding 24m, staircases must be mechanically pressurized to 50 Pa to prevent toxic smoke from infiltrating during an evacuation.
  • 3. Locating Underground Fire Tanks Under Building Plinth: CFO norms mandate that the dedicated underground fire static water reservoir must be located outside the building footprint to allow uninterrupted fire engine suction connections.
  • 4. Placing Cantilever Refuge Slabs Over Entry Drives Without Headroom: When refuge platforms cantilever out over the 6.0m fire driveway, they must maintain a minimum clear vertical height of 4.50 meters so fire tenders are not obstructed.
  • 5. Calculating Refuge Capacity for Only 1 Floor: Scrutiny engineers will reject plans if the refuge area is only sized for the immediate floor. The area must accommodate the full cumulative occupancy of all 7 intermediate floors.
