Off-Street Parking & Circulation Standards

Master vehicular parking design under Chapter 8: bay dimensions for cars, two-wheelers, and transport trucks; tenement-based residential quotas; mandatory 10% visitor additions; and basement ramp gradient limits (1:10).

01 // DIMENSIONAL ENVELOPES

Statutory Vehicle Stall Dimensions (Reg. 8.1.1)

FOUR-WHEELER (CAR)
2.5m × 5.0m

Minimum area 12.5 sq.m. Clear driving aisle ≥ 3.0m (one-way) or 6.0m (two-way).

TWO-WHEELER (SCOOTER)
1.0m × 2.0m

Minimum area 2.0 sq.m. Provided at 2 spaces per tenement.

BICYCLE
0.7m × 1.4m

Minimum area 1.0 sq.m for non-motorized transport.

LOADING / TRANSPORT
3.75m × 7.5m

For commercial markets, warehouses, and freight unloading.
