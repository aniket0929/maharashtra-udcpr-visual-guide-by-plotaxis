TDR Generation Multipliers, Indexation & Credit Notes

Master the economics and mechanics of Transferable Development Rights (TDR) in Maharashtra: land surrender generation multipliers (2.00x non-congested vs 3.00x congested), amenity construction bonuses, the statutory indexation formula $X = (R_g / R_r) imes Y$, and Reservation Credit Certificates (RCC).

01 // STATUTORY LIFECYCLE

How TDR is Born, Valued, and Consumed

Transferable Development Rights allow land owners to surrender public reservations (DP roads, schools, gardens) without cash compensation from municipal coffers, receiving tradable certificates instead:

Stage 1 • Surrender
Land Handover

Land owner hands over unencumbered possession of reserved plot to the Planning Authority with 7/12 mutation in municipal name.

Stage 2 • Multiplier
2.00x or 3.00x DRC

DRC face value = 2.00 × Land Area (Non-congested) or 3.00 × Land Area (Congested Gaothan). +0.50x if amenity is built.

Stage 3 • Indexation
ASR Rate Adjustment

When loaded onto a receiving plot with different land value, BUA is adjusted via ratio of Ready Reckoner rates: $X = (R_g / R_r) imes Y$.

Stage 4 • Utilization
Road Width Capping

TDR can only be loaded on receiving roads ≥ 12.0m, up to the maximum road tier caps established in Table 6-A (max 0.65 FSI).

03 // STATUTORY RESTRICTIONS

Where TDR Can and Cannot Be Utilized (Regulation 11.2.6)

  • 1. Strictly Forbidden on Roads Under 12.0m: Under Table 6-A, maximum permissible TDR loading is 0.00 on roads narrower than 12.0 meters. Any attempt to load TDR on 9.0m or 7.5m roads will be rejected immediately.
  • 2. Forbidden in Congested Core (Gaothan) Areas: Under Reg. 11.2, TDR generated from any source cannot be utilized in congested (gaothan) areas, as Gaothans lack adequate infrastructure to absorb external density.
  • 3. Forbidden in Coastal Regulation Zones (CRZ) & Eco-Sensitive Zones: TDR utilization is strictly barred inside CRZ-I, CRZ-II, and buffer zones surrounding national parks and wildlife sanctuaries.
  • 4. Heritage TDR Priority: Heritage TDR generated from the conservation of Grade-I and Grade-II heritage structures can be loaded on any receiving plot where standard TDR is permissible, without geographical sub-zone restrictions.
  • 5. TDR Cannot Exceed Table 6-A Caps: Even if an owner holds 10,000 sq.m of DRC certificates, they can only consume the maximum TDR cap specified for their abutting road width (e.g. 0.40 on 18m road, 0.65 on 30m road).
