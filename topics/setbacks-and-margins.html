Marginal Open Spaces & High-Rise Clearances

How to determine required front, side, and rear marginal setbacks for any structure. Master the difference between Congested (Gaothan) and Non-Congested rules, the $H/5$ height setback formula, and mandatory 6.0m fire tender driveways.

01 // REGULATORY LOGIC

The Three Critical Setback Envelopes

  • Front Road Setback (Reg. 6.2.1): Measured from the proposed road widening line to the exterior face of the building. Dictated primarily by the statutory width of the abutting street (e.g. 3.0m on roads < 12m, 4.5m on roads 12–24m, 6.0m on roads ≥ 24m).
  • Side & Rear Margins for Low-Rise (≤ 15.0m): Fixed minimum clearances (typically 1.5m to 3.0m depending on plot size and occupancy) ensuring light, privacy, and natural ventilation under Table 6-C.
  • Side & Rear Margins for High-Rise (> 15.0m): Governed by the height formula:
    $$\text{Margin} = \frac{H}{5}$$
    Where $H$ is total building height. Crucially, once a building triggers special building or high-rise classification (> 15m or > 24m), a clear unobstructed driveway of at least 6.0 meters must be maintained all around the building for emergency fire engine access.
