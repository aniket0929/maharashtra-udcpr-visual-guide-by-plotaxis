Marginal Open Spaces & High-Rise Setback Clearances

Determine exact front, side, and rear marginal setbacks for any building in Maharashtra. Master low-rise minimums (Table 6-C), the H/5 high-rise rule (Reg. 6.2.3), congested gaothan exemptions, and the mandatory 6.0m clear fire tender driveway envelope.

01 // PEDAGOGICAL BREAKDOWN

The Three Critical Setback Envelopes

  • 1. Front Road-Side Margin (Reg. 6.2.1): Measured from the sanctioned road widening boundary to the outer structural wall. Dictated strictly by abutting road width: 3.0m (roads < 12m), 4.5m (roads 12m to < 24m), and 6.0m (roads ≥ 24m). In congested gaothans, reduced to 1.50m.
  • 2. Low-Rise Side & Rear Open Space (Height ≤ 15.0m): Table 6-C governs based on plot size and occupancy. Typically 1.5m to 3.0m, ensuring natural ventilation, light, and privacy separation between adjacent land parcels.
  • 3. High-Rise Side & Rear Margin (Height > 15.0m): Governed by the statutory height formula:
    $$ ext{Margin} = rac{H}{5}$$
    Where $H$ is the total building height from ground level to terrace slab. Crucially, once height exceeds 15.0m or 24.0m, an unobstructed 6.0m hard-paved fire driveway must wrap around the building for emergency firefighting access.

04 // SCRUTINY WATCHOUTS

Critical Plan Scrutiny Pitfalls for Margins & Fire Paths

  • 1. Encroaching the 6.0m Fire Tender Path with Surface Parking: Reg. 6.2.3 and CFO guidelines strictly prohibit open surface parking bays within the mandatory 6.0m fire tender driveway. All visitors and resident open parking must be placed outside this 6.0m corridor.
  • 2. Insufficient Turning Radius at Building Corners: CFO scrutiny requires a minimum 9.0m inner radius and 12.0m outer turning radius at all building corners so heavy hydraulic turntable ladder fire trucks can maneuver without hitting plinths or compound walls.
  • 3. Low Overhead Balconies & Canopies: While balconies may project up to 1.50m (Reg. 9.13), any projection extending over the fire driveway must maintain a minimum clear vertical height of 4.50 meters from driveway level.
  • 4. Fire Driveway Structural Load Bearing Failure: Paving over soft soil without structural reinforcement triggers CFO rejection. The fire driveway must be engineered to support a minimum axle load of 45 metric tonnes.
  • 5. Misinterpreting H/5 for Mixed Occupancy: When commercial podiums (e.g. 12m height) support a residential tower (e.g. 45m height), the setback for the podium can follow low-rise rules, but the upper tower must step back to satisfy the full $H/5 = 9.0 ext{m}$ margin from the plot boundary.
