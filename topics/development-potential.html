Mastering Development Potential & FSI Computation

How to determine the maximum permissible built-up area for any plot in Maharashtra. Understand the four stacking layers of building potential under Regulation 6.3: Basic FSI, Premium FSI, TDR Loading, and Ancillary FSI.

01 // MECHANISM

The Four Stacking Layers of Potential

Unlike older municipal by-laws with a single flat FSI number, UDCPR uses an additive stacking system where road width governs how high your building potential can climb:

Layer 1
Basic FSI

Available as of right without extra government charges (1.10 for most Municipal Corporations).

Layer 2
Premium FSI

Purchased directly from the Planning Authority at 35% of the prevailing ASR land rate.

Layer 3
TDR Loading

Purchased from the open market as DRC certificates generated from surrendered lands.

Layer 4
Ancillary Area FSI

Up to 60% (residential) or 80% (commercial) extra area for flowerbeds and balconies at premium.
