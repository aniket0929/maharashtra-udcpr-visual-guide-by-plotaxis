Layout Planning, Land Subdivision & Net Plot Area

Master the statutory partitioning of large land holdings under Chapter 3. Understand how Development Plan (DP) road deductions, internal access roads (Table 3-A vs 3-C), 10% Recreational Open Space (ROS), 5% Amenity Space, and 20% Inclusive Housing shape the final Net Plot Area available for FSI consumption.

01 // PEDAGOGICAL BREAKDOWN

The Step-by-Step Land Partition Sequence

A surveyor begins with the Gross Holding Area from 7/12 land records. Before any building can be designed, the land must pass through four statutory partitions:

Step 1 • DP Deductions
Road Widening & Reservations

Land affected by DP roads or public reservations must be surrendered to the Authority (compensated via FSI / TDR under Reg. 11.1).

Step 2 • 10% ROS
Recreational Open Space

Mandatory on layouts > 0.40 Ha (4,000 sqm). Min 400 sqm and min 10m dimension. Crucially: Not deducted from Net Plot FSI!

Step 3 • 5% Amenity
Public Amenity Space

Mandatory on layouts > 2.0 Ha (20,000 sqm) in Municipal areas. Surrendered to Corporation or constructed in-situ for civic uses.

Step 4 • 20% Inclusive
Social Housing Quota

On plots ≥ 4,000 sqm, 20% of basic FSI or 20% of plots (30–50 sqm) must be constructed for EWS/LIG families (Reg. 3.8).

04 // SCRUTINY WATCHOUTS

Top 5 Layout Scrutiny Traps Triggering Plan Rejection

  • 1. Deducting 10% Recreational Open Space from Net Plot Area: Under Regulation 3.9(ii), Recreational Open Space (ROS) is NEVER deducted when computing Net Plot Area for FSI. An architect deducting ROS loses 10% of their client's legitimate building potential.
  • 2. Fragmenting ROS into Unusable Pockets: Under Reg. 3.4.1, no recreational open space pocket can measure less than 400 sq.m, and the minimum dimension along any side cannot be less than 10.0 meters. Providing residual slivers or strips between buildings is strictly non-compliant.
  • 3. Placing Club House Outside 15% Ground Coverage Limit: While a G+1 club house / gym is permissible in the ROS, its ground coverage cannot exceed 15% of the ROS area, and it must maintain a 3.0m margin from ROS boundaries.
  • 4. Violating Cul-de-Sac Turnaround Dimensions: Dead-end internal layout roads must provide a turning circle with a minimum 9.0m radius (or $9.0 ext{m} imes 9.0 ext{m}$ hammerhead) so emergency fire tenders and municipal garbage trucks can turn without reversing.
  • 5. Evading 20% Inclusive Housing on 4,000+ sqm Plots: Attempting to artificially fragment a single 6,000 sqm survey number into smaller plots to bypass the 4,000 sqm Inclusive Housing threshold (Reg. 3.8) triggers automatic revocation under MRTP Section 51.
