FSI Stacking, Premium FSI & TDR Building Potential

Demystify the four-tiered FSI stacking pyramid of UDCPR: Basic FSI, Premium FSI at 35% ASR, Transferable Development Rights (TDR) loading caps, and Note (xiv) net-versus-gross area base rules.

01 // EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Plain-Language Executive Summary

In Maharashtra under UDCPR-2020, total buildable potential on any plot is never a single arbitrary number. Instead, it is constructed through a rigid, statutory four-tier FSI Stacking Architecture governed primarily by Table 6-A for Congested (Core/Gaothan) areas and Table 6-G for Non-Congested (Outside Core) areas.

The base is Basic FSI, which is available by right without premium (1.10 in non-congested areas, 1.50 to 2.00 in congested areas). Above this base sits Premium FSI, purchased directly from the Planning Authority at strictly 35% of the unguided Annual Statement of Rates (ASR) land value. Above premium FSI sits TDR Loading, requiring certificates procured from the market (with a statutory mandate under Note (v) that 30% to 50% must be Slum or Urban Renewal / Amenity TDR).

Crucially, under Note (xiv) to Table 6-G (clarified dt. 17th Sept 2021 and 2nd Dec 2021), Basic FSI is calculated strictly on the net plot remaining with the owner after deducting surrendered DP roads, reservations, and amenity space. In contrast, Premium FSI and TDR potential are calculated on the entire gross plot area prior to surrender!

TIER 1 • BASE

Basic FSI Entitlement

Basic FSI is free of premium. In non-congested areas, it is universally 1.10 across all road widths (Table 6-G Col 3). In congested gaothan areas, it is 1.50 on roads < 9.0m, jumping to 2.00 on roads ≥ 9.0m (Table 6-A Col 3). Computed strictly on the net remaining plot after DP road and amenity surrender.

TIER 2 • PAID LEVY

Premium FSI @ 35% ASR

Permissible on roads ≥ 9.0m. In Municipal Corporations and CIDCO NTDA, premium FSI is a flat 0.50 for all roads ≥ 9.0m. In Municipal Councils and RP areas, it is capped at 0.30. Purchased directly from the local authority at strictly 35% of unguided ASR land rate.

TIER 3 • MARKET TDR

TDR Loading & Slum Quota

Scales progressively with road width: from 0.40 on 9.0m roads up to 1.40 on ≥ 30.0m roads in Corporations. Note (v) mandates that minimum 30% and maximum 50% of loaded TDR must be Slum TDR or Urban Renewal / Amenity TDR. TDR is not permitted where no Planning Authority exists.

SPECIAL CONDITIONS

Access & Regularization Rules

Point Access (Note xii): Full potential is permissible on dead-end roads only if length does not exceed 100 m.
Gunthewari Plots (Note xi): Regularized plots get full Basic/Premium FSI, but TDR loading is strictly reduced to 50%.
Subdivided plots ≤ 0.4 Ha (Note ix): Basic & Premium are 75%, TDR is 50%.

04 // WORKED PRACTICAL EXAMPLE

Worked Practical Calculation

Worked Case: Building Potential Computation under Table 6-G & Note (xiv)

Site Parameters:

  • Gross Plot Area = 2,000 sq.m in a Municipal Corporation area.
  • Fronting Road Width = 18.0 meters (falls in 15.0m to < 24.0m tier).
  • Area surrendered to Corporation for 18.0m DP road widening = 200 sq.m.
  • ASR Land Rate for the parcel = Rs 25,000 / sq.m.

Step 1: Net Plot vs Gross Plot Allocation (Note xiv)

Net Retained Plot Area = $2,000 - 200 = \mathbf{1,800\text{ sq.m}}$.

Basic FSI is calculated strictly on the net remaining plot: $$\text{Basic BUA} = 1,800\text{ sq.m} \times 1.10 = \mathbf{1,980\text{ sq.m}}$$

Step 2: Premium FSI on Gross Area (Table 6-G, Col 4 & Note xiv)

Under Note (xiv), Premium FSI potential applies to the gross plot area: $$\text{Permissible Premium FSI} = 0.50$$ $$\text{Premium BUA} = 2,000\text{ sq.m} \times 0.50 = \mathbf{1,000\text{ sq.m}}$$ $$\text{Cost of Premium FSI} = 1,000\text{ sq.m} \times (0.35 \times \text{Rs } 25,000) = \mathbf{\text{Rs } 8,750,000}$$

Step 3: TDR Loading Capacity (Table 6-G, Col 5 & Note xiv)

TDR potential applies to the gross plot area: $$\text{Permissible TDR Factor} = 0.90$$ $$\text{Maximum TDR BUA} = 2,000\text{ sq.m} \times 0.90 = \mathbf{1,800\text{ sq.m}}$$

Under Note (v), mandatory Slum / Urban Renewal TDR quota (30% to 50%): $$\text{Mandatory Slum TDR (min 30%)} = 1,800 \times 0.30 = \mathbf{540\text{ sq.m}}$$ $$\text{General / Reserved TDR (balance 70%)} = 1,800 \times 0.70 = \mathbf{1,260\text{ sq.m}}$$

Total Statutory Built-Up Potential (Before Ancillary FSI):

Total BUA = $1,980\text{ (Basic)} + 1,000\text{ (Premium)} + 1,800\text{ (TDR)} = \mathbf{4,780\text{ sq.m}}$.

Effective FSI on net retained plot = $4,780 / 1,800 = \mathbf{2.655}$. (Notice how Note (xiv) gross loading elevates the effective potential on the remaining parcel!)

05 // SCRUTINY WATCHOUTS

Common Pitfalls & Sanction Watchouts

CRITICAL STATUTORY COMPLIANCE PITFALLS: REGULATION 6.1 & 6.3

  • Miscalculating Note (xiv) Base Areas: Applying the Basic FSI of 1.10 to the gross plot area prior to DP road surrender is illegal and will be rejected during scrutiny. Basic FSI is only on the net plot retained.
  • Ignoring the 100m Point Access Limit (Note xii): If your plot accesses a wide public road via a private or dead-end approach less than the required table width, full Table 6-G potential is allowed only if the length of that point access does not exceed 100 meters. Exceeding 100m demotes the building potential to the width of the approach road itself!
  • Violating the Slum TDR Sub-Quota (Note v): Attempting to load 100% General or Agricultural Reservation TDR without procuring the mandatory 30% to 50% Slum / Urban Renewal TDR will block final Occupancy Certificate issuance.
  • Assuming Roads < 9.0m Allow TDR or Premium: Under both Table 6-A and Table 6-G, road widths below 9.0m have strictly nil Premium FSI and nil TDR loading. The plot is strictly locked to Basic FSI (1.10 or 1.50).
06 // AMENDMENT HISTORY

Amendment & Clarification History (#)

Statutory Evolution & Government Orders

  • Addendum CR 236/18 (dt. 14 Jan 2021): Clarified non-applicability of ancillary FSI to SRA rehabilitation component.
  • Letter Clarification CR 236/18 Part-2 (dt. 17 Sept 2021): Confirmed Note (xiv) allowing entire gross plot area for Premium FSI + TDR calculation, preserving net plot restriction for Basic FSI.
  • Addendum CR 236/18 Part-1 (dt. 08 Oct 2021): Added flexibility for Planning Authorities to mandate equal-proportionate consumption of Premium FSI and TDR via reasoned speaking orders.
  • Corrigendum CR 121/21 (dt. 02 Dec 2021): Formalized inclusion of Urban Renewal Project (URP) and Amenity construction TDR under the 30%–50% mandatory tranche in Note (v).
  • Notification CR 236/18 Part-6 (dt. 12 Oct 2022): Formally extended the Municipal Corporation FSI columns in Tables 6-A and 6-G to CIDCO acting as Planning Authority (NTDA).
