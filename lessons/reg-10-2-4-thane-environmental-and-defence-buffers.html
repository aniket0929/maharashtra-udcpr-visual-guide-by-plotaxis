Thane Hazard Zones, Defence Envelopes & Yeur Forest

Due to its legacy as a major industrial manufacturing belt interlocked with critical national defense bases and the protected wilderness of Sanjay Gandhi National Park, Thane possesses some of the most rigorous buffer regulations in India. Regulations 10.2.4 through 10.2.8 establish concentric safety envelopes around hazardous chemical industries (a mandatory 100 m non-habitable Green Belt coupled with a 150 m Low Density Belt capped at 0.50 FSI and G+2 storeys), codify the Air Force Station 100 m security perimeter, enforce color-coded radial height caps around the Kolshet/Kavesar naval defense installation, and enforce stringent low-intensity controls across Yeur Village (G-1 Zone) and the adjoining G-3 Forest Zone.

01 // EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Plain-Language Executive Summary

Development in peripheral Thane requires rigorous site scrutiny against environmental hazards and national security perimeters. Key statutory protections include:

  • Hazardous Chemical Industries Concentric Rings (Reg 10.2.4):
    • Inner 100 m Green Belt: Measured from the factory boundary. Zero new construction or reconstruction is permitted; only repairs to authorized structures are tolerated. Cost of land acquisition is borne by the chemical industry, with landowners compensated via TDR.
    • Intermediate 150 m Low Density Zone: Extends from 100 m to 250 m from the industry boundary. Maximum permissible FSI is capped at 0.50, and building height is restricted to Ground + 2 storeys only.
    • Outer Zone (> 250 m to 1,000 m): Development is permitted as per standard UDCPR norms.
    • Automatic Sunset (Suo-Moto Cessation): If the chemical industry permanently closes or shifts (certified by the Director of Industries / DISH), both the 100 m Green Belt and the 150 m Low Density Zone suo-moto cease to exist, restoring normal zoning potential.
  • Air Force Station Thane (Reg 10.2.5): Under Ministry of Defence SRO 4 (2010), a strict 100.0 m restricted zone is enforced from the outer parapet of the Air Force base. Within this 100 m zone, no construction, no planting of live hedges/orchards, and no ground excavation or level changes are permitted. Beyond 100 m, no restrictions or Air Force NOC are required.
  • Kolshet / Kavesar / Waghbil Defence Base (Reg 10.2.6): Radial color-coded height limit zones around the naval defense establishment:
    • Red & Brown Zones: Red (Unit Area) = Nil; Brown (Buffer Zone) = Nil (No development).
    • North & West Sectors: Orange: 18 m • Pink: 27 m • Navy Blue: 32 m • Yellow: 46 m • Violet: 64 m • Green: 84 m.
    • East & South-East Sectors: Orange: 15 m • Pink: 20 m • Yellow: 35 m • Violet: 40 m • Sky Blue: 45 m.
  • Yeur Village (G-1 Zone) & Forest (G-3 Zone) (Reg 10.2.7 & 10.2.8): Special green protections preserving the fragile eco-sensitive boundary of Sanjay Gandhi National Park, prohibiting high-density commercial exploitation.
02 // STATUTORY RULE

Statutory Rule & Clause Breakdown

UDCPR Reg. 10.2.4 to 10.2.8 STATUTORY PROVISION

"10.2.4 Development around Hazardous Industries (Chemical Zone Areas): a) Green Belt - i) There shall be green belt of 100 m. around the boundary of the Hazardous Chemical Industries... iii) The cost of acquisition of land for the green belt... will be borne by the Industry / Industry Association. The private land owner will be given Transfer of Development Rights based on FSI for the present land usage. iv) The existing authorised structures should be tolerated. However, only repairs to such existing structures should be allowed in future and no reconstruction or new construction should be allowed in the Green Belt. b) Creation of Low Density Zone - i) Beyond the Green Belt, another belt of 150 m. should be created as "Low Density Zone"... ii) The FSI in the Low Density Zone shall be 0.5 and only Ground plus two storey structures should be allowed. d) Other Stipulations - ii) Upon certification by the Director of Industries / DISH that the Chemical Industry has been permanently closed or has shifted elsewhere, the 100 m. Green Belt and the further 150 m. low density belt... shall suo-moto cease to exist. 10.2.5 Development around Air Force Stations: i) As per Government of India, MoD, SRO No. 4, dt. 13th January, 2010... Air Force Station Thane falls within the 100 m. restriction zone. As per notification, no construction should be permitted within the restricted zone of 100 m. from the outer parapet... live hedges, tree/rows or clumps of trees or orchards shall not be maintained... underground structure, wherein no digging and / or change in the level of ground is permitted. ii) Beyond the notified zone of 100 m., no restriction is required... and no N.O.C. from Air Force Station, Thane is necessary. 10.2.6 Development around Defence Establishment (Kolshet / Kavesar / Waghbil): Height restrictions to future constructions: (a) North, NW, West and SW sides: Red/Brown (Nil), Orange (18m), Pink (27m), Navy Blue (32m), Yellow (46m), Violet (64m), Green (84m). (b) North East, East and South East sides: Red/Brown (Nil), Orange (15m), Pink (20m), Yellow (35m), Violet (40m), Sky Blue (45m)."

01

100m Chemical Green Belt (Reg 10.2.4.a)

A non-habitable 100.0 m green belt encircles hazardous chemical factories. Zero new construction or reconstruction is permitted; only structural repairs to tolerated buildings are allowed. Private owners receive TDR.

02

150m Low Density Zone (Reg 10.2.4.b)

Extends from 100 m to 250 m radial distance. Development is restricted to a maximum FSI of 0.50 and a height of Ground + 2 storeys only. The zone automatically dissolves upon factory closure.

03

Air Force Station 100m Perimeter (Reg 10.2.5)

Strict MoD 100.0 m restricted zone from outer parapet. Complete ban on construction, tree rows/orchards, and underground excavations. Beyond 100 m, no restrictions or Air Force NOC apply.

04

Kolshet Naval Base Height Plates (Reg 10.2.6)

Color-coded radial height rings protect defense radar sightlines: Red/Brown (0 m), Orange (15-18 m), Pink (20-27 m), Yellow (35-46 m), Violet (40-64 m), and Green (84 m).

04 // WORKED PRACTICAL EXAMPLE

Worked Practical Calculation

PRACTICAL SCRUTINY CALCULATION: INDUSTRIAL SAFETY RADIALS & SHIFTING RULES

Scenario 1: Development Proposal at 180 m from Chemical Plant

A landowner in Thane holds a 2,500 sq.m plot situated 180 meters from the boundary of an operational chemical manufacturing factory. The applicant submits plans for a G+7 residential apartment building proposing 1.50 FSI. Can this be sanctioned?

Step 1: Determine Statutory Radial Zone (Reg 10.2.4)
  • Radial distance = $180\text{ m}$.
  • 0 to 100 m = Green Belt (Nil development).
  • 100 to 250 m = Low Density Zone. The plot lies directly inside this zone.
Step 2: Scrutinize Permissible FSI & Storey Limits
  • Under Regulation 10.2.4(b)(ii): Maximum permissible FSI is capped at 0.50.
  • Maximum Permissible BUA: $2,500\text{ sq.m} \times 0.50 = \mathbf{1,250\text{ sq.m}}$. The proposed 1.50 FSI (3,750 sq.m) is unlawful.
  • Storey Restriction: Strictly limited to Ground plus two storeys (G+2). The proposed G+7 is rejected.

Scenario 2: Factory Relocation & Suo-Moto Dissolution (Reg 10.2.4.d.ii)

Step 3: Verification of Closure Certificate
  • When the chemical plant permanently closes and obtains certification from DISH (Director of Industrial Safety and Health), the 100 m Green Belt and 150 m Low Density Belt suo-moto cease to exist.
  • Once certified, the plot immediately regains normal residential zoning potential under Chapter 6 without requiring a formal MRTP Section 37 Development Plan modification.
05 // SCRUTINY WATCHOUTS

Common Pitfalls & Sanction Watchouts

COMMON SANCTION & SCRUTINY PITFALLS IN THANE HAZARD & DEFENSE ZONES

  • Demanding Air Force NOC Beyond 100 Meters: Scrutiny officers frequently demand defense NOCs for plots located at 120 m or 200 m from Air Force Station Thane. Reg 10.2.5(ii) explicitly clarifies that beyond 100 m, no NOC is necessary.
  • Excavating Basements within 100 m of Air Force Base: Proposing underground basements or borewells inside the 100 m Air Force ring. Reg 10.2.5(i) explicitly bans any digging or change in ground level.
  • Approving Reconstruction in Chemical Green Belt: Attempting to demolish and reconstruct an existing building inside the 100 m chemical green belt. Reg 10.2.4(a)(iv) tolerates existing buildings for repairs only—new construction or reconstruction is prohibited.
  • Overlooking Color-Coded Radar Envelopes at Kolshet: Submitting 30 m tall towers in the Orange or Pink zones of Kolshet/Kavesar. Permissible heights are strictly tied to the notification map (15m–27m limits).
