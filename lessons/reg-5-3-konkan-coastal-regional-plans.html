Konkan Coastal Regional Plans: Ratnagiri, Sindhudurg & Raigad

Master the delicate balance between environmental conservation and economic development along Maharashtra's 720-km coastline: the Sindhudurg Tourism Master Plan zones (T-1 to T-5), Coastal Regulation Zone (CRZ) overlays, low-impact eco-resorts, orchard protections in Ratnagiri, and Raigad coastal wadi regulations within 1 km of the sea.

01 // EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Plain-Language Executive Summary

Maharashtra's Konkan coastline—stretching across Raigad, Ratnagiri, and Sindhudurg—is globally celebrated for its biodiversity, pristine beaches, and Alphonso mango horticulture. Regulations 5.3 and 5.7 establish customized regional planning controls tailored specifically to Konkan's coastal terrain.

1. Sindhudurg Tourism Master Plan & T-Zones (Reg. 5.3.2)

Recognized as India's first tourism district, Sindhudurg is governed by specialized Tourism Zones (T-1 to T-5):

Tourism Zone Code Geographic Focus / Orientation Permissible Tourism & Built Uses Governing FSI & Heights
Zone T-1 & T-2 Beachfront & Watersport Corridors Beach resorts, cottages, scuba centers, homestays 0.20 to 0.50 FSI; max Ground + 1 floor (≤ 9.0m)
Zone T-3 Historic Fort & Cultural Environs Heritage hotels, museums, handicraft bazaars Strict architectural heritage harmony
Zone T-4 & T-5 Hinterland Agro-Tourism & Coastal Belts Farm tours, cashew/spice processing, eco-villas 0.20 Basic FSI + up to 0.50 on premium

2. CRZ Overlays & Orchard Protections (Reg. 5.3.1 & 5.3.2(b))

  • CRZ Supremacy: All permissions in coastal belts remain subordinate to the Coastal Regulation Zone (CRZ) Notification issued by the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC). Construction within 200m or 500m of the High Tide Line (HTL) requires prior MCZMA recommendation.
  • Orchard & Plantation Preservation: Mature mango, coconut, and betel-nut groves are protected. Layouts in agricultural zones of Ratnagiri must retain at least 70% of mature fruit-bearing trees.
  • 200m Gaothan Radius: In Sindhudurg, natural gaothan expansion is strictly limited to 200 meters from the gaothan boundary.

3. Raigad Coastal Belt & Wadi Lands (Reg. 5.7)

  • Coastal 1 km Belt (Reg. 5.7.2): Defined as the land area lying within 1.0 kilometer from the sea coast. Development in traditional "wadi lands" (horticultural coconut/arecanut homesteads) is restricted to low-intensity vernacular dwellings to prevent high-density commercialization.
  • Growth Centers (Reg. 5.7.3): In Rajpuri and Usar growth centers, low-density zones are subject to a maximum 0.50 FSI with generous garden offsets.

REG. 5.3.2 // SINDHUDURG TOURISM

T-Zone Framework

Tailored for eco-resorts, scuba homestays, and farm tourism. Enforces low-rise G+1 height limits to preserve the coastal silhouette.

REG. 5.7.2 // RAIGAD 1KM BELT

Coastal Wadi Safeguard

Defines the 1 km coastal strip from the sea. Restricts construction in traditional coconut/betel-nut wadis to preserve coastal ecology.

04 // WORKED PRACTICAL EXAMPLE

Worked Practical Calculation

Practical Scrutiny Problem: An architect prepares plans for a beach resort on a 10,000 sq.m plot in Malvan (Sindhudurg District) located in Tourism Zone T-2.

CRZ LINE AUDIT
The plot is located 300m from the High Tide Line (HTL), falling in CRZ-III (No Development Zone up to 200m; 200m to 500m developable).
Under Regulation 5.3.2(b), development is strictly subject to prior MCZMA (Maharashtra Coastal Zone Management Authority) CRZ clearance before building sanction.
FSI & HEIGHT LIMITS
Basic FSI permissible = 0.20 → 2,000 sq.m BUA.
Additional FSI up to 0.50 (3,000 sq.m) is available upon paying premium @ 20% of ASR land rate.
Maximum height is restricted to Ground + 1 upper floor (9.0 meters) with sloping Konkan mangalore-tile roofs to harmonize with local landscape.
✓ STATUTORY RESULT: Sited outside 200m NDZ, approved at 0.50 FSI with G+1 height limit.
05 // SCRUTINY WATCHOUTS

Common Pitfalls & Sanction Watchouts

Pitfall 1: Overlooking the 1 km Coastal Belt Definition in Raigad

Under Regulation 5.7.2, the coastal belt is explicitly defined as the area within 1.0 km from the sea. Developers mistakenly assume that beyond 500m (the typical CRZ limit), normal urban high-density rules apply. In Raigad, the 1 km wadi restrictions apply regardless of CRZ distance!

Pitfall 2: Demanding 500m Gaothan Expansion in Sindhudurg

Regulation 5.3.2(xi) explicitly caps natural expansion of gaothans in Sindhudurg to 200 meters from the gaothan boundary. Demanding or planning for 500m expansion under the general rules of Reg. 5.1.1 is illegal in Sindhudurg.
