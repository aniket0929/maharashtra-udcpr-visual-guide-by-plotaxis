District-Specific Regional Plans: Kolhapur, LIGO Hingoli & Marathwada

Learn the specialized regional regulations governing distinct territorial and scientific hubs across Maharashtra: Kolhapur Regional Plan (Reg. 5.4) committed textile developments, the world-class LIGO-India Gravitational Wave Observatory vibration buffers in Hingoli and Marathwada (Reg. 5.6), Department of Atomic Energy clearances, and Aurangabad Regional Plan provisions.

01 // EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Plain-Language Executive Summary

Certain districts in Maharashtra host specialized mega-science facilities, historic manufacturing hubs, and unique agricultural belts. Regulations 5.4, 5.6, 5.8, and 5.12 provide dedicated regulatory carve-outs to address these distinct requirements.

1. Kolhapur Regional Plan & Ichalkaranji Textile Belt (Reg. 5.4)

Recognized as the textile capital of Western Maharashtra, Kolhapur's regional regulations establish special protections for committed developments:

  • Layouts approved prior to publication of the Draft Regional Plan in Ichalkaranji and Kolhapur continue to be valid.
  • Owners can either execute earlier approvals in-toto or upgrade to UDCPR standards without paying additional conversion premiums.

2. LIGO-India Observatory & Atomic Energy Zones: Hingoli, Buldhana, Washim (Reg. 5.6)

Strategic Scientific Buffer: Hingoli district is the chosen site for LIGO-India (Laser Interferometer Gravitational-Wave Observatory), one of only three such ultra-sensitive scientific instruments on Earth:

  • Because gravitational wave detectors measure ripples in spacetime smaller than a fraction of an atomic nucleus, ground vibrations and industrial noise must be strictly zeroed out.
  • Development within surrounding peripheral buffers must comply with specialized vibrational, electromagnetic, and acoustic restrictions formulated by the Department of Atomic Energy (DAE).
  • Quarrying, heavy blasting, and high-vibration manufacturing are strictly banned in proximity to the observatory site.

3. Aurangabad Regional Plan (Reg. 5.12)

Provides customized land use provisions for the Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar (Aurangabad) regional territory, balancing the DMIC (Delhi-Mumbai Industrial Corridor / AURIC smart city) industrial growth with heritage monument buffer corridors (Ajanta-Ellora caves).

02 // STATUTORY RULE

Statutory Rule & Clause Breakdown

UDCPR Reg. 5.4, 5.6 & 5.12 STATUTORY PROVISION

"5.4 FOR KOLHAPUR REGIONAL PLAN: 5.4.1 Committed Development: Following regulations shall be applicable for committed development... prior to the publication of the Draft Regional Plan... 5.6 FOR HINGOLI, BULDHANA, WASHIM, YAVATMAL, NANDED REGIONAL PLAN: Following additional regulations shall be applicable for the development in the areas affected by the project of Department of Atomic Energy / LIGO... b) The DCR as amended from time to time, by the Department of Atomic Energy shall be applicable... 5.12 AURANGABAD REGIONAL PLAN."

REG. 5.6 // LIGO-INDIA BUFFER

Mega-Science Safeguard

Enforces strict seismic and vibrational quiet zones around Hingoli's gravitational wave observatory in accordance with Department of Atomic Energy norms.

REG. 5.4 // TEXTILE COMMITMENTS

Kolhapur / Ichalkaranji

Guarantees grandfathered protection for pre-RP industrial and residential layouts, facilitating powerloom expansion without penalty.

04 // WORKED PRACTICAL EXAMPLE

Worked Practical Calculation

Practical Scrutiny Problem: An entrepreneur proposes a stone-crushing quarry unit in Aundha Nagnath taluka (Hingoli District) within the buffer perimeter of the LIGO-India scientific project.

DAE CLEARANCE AUDIT
Under Regulation 5.6(b): "The Development Control Regulations as amended from time to time, by the Department of Atomic Energy shall be applicable" for development in areas affected by DAE/LIGO projects.
Because stone crushing involves high-energy ground vibrations and heavy blast shocks, it directly impairs gravitational wave laser interferometry.
SCRUTINY DETERMINATION
The proposal requires mandatory prior scrutiny and clearance from the Department of Atomic Energy. Without explicit DAE NOC, the District Collectorate must reject the quarry license.
✓ STATUTORY RESULT: Application returned for mandatory DAE scientific clearance.
05 // SCRUTINY WATCHOUTS

Common Pitfalls & Sanction Watchouts

Pitfall 1: Establishing Heavy Blasting Operations Near LIGO Hingoli

Mining and quarry operators often purchase rural land in Hingoli expecting standard MLRC mining sanction. Under Regulation 5.6, DAE security and seismic rules override general quarry permissions in the observatory corridor.
