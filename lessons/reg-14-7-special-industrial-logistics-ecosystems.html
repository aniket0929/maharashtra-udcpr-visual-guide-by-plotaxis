Special Industrial, Logistics & Eco-Tourism Ecosystems

To position Maharashtra as India's primary powerhouse for high-technology industries, global supply chain corridors, and ecological conservation, Regulations 14.9 through 14.13 establish specialized territorial development frameworks. These encompass: Eco-Tourism around National Parks (Reg 14.9 - strict 10% built-up cap and porous chainlink fencing for wildlife corridors), Integrated Information Technology Townships (IITP, Reg 14.10 - minimum 10 acres with 2.50 FSI, deemed NA conversion, and 140 lpcd water security), Integrated Logistics Parks (ILP, Reg 14.11 - minimum 5 acres on 15m roads with up to 200% additional industrial FSI and 24m warehouse heights), Aerospace and Defense Manufacturing Townships (Reg 14.12 - granting +0.50 FSI bonuses for R&D centers), and MIDC Integrated Industrial Areas (Reg 14.13).

01 // EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Plain-Language Executive Summary

These specialized statutory frameworks enable targeted economic development by cutting bureaucratic red tape, granting deemed industrial conversions, and unlocking higher floor space for strategic sectors:

  • Eco-Tourism & Wildlife Buffer Resorts (Reg 14.9):
    • Applicability: Private lands within 5 km (or notified Eco-Sensitive Zone boundary) of National Parks and Wildlife Sanctuaries. Minimum land: 1.0 Hectare.
    • Built-up Limits: Maximum BUA capped at 10% of gross plot area with G+1 structure (max 9.0m height). One observation tower up to 15.0m height permitted.
    • Wildlife Movement Guarantee: Fortification/fencing allowed for only 10% of land around buildings using chain link without masonry walls, leaving 90% completely unobstructed for wild animals.
    • Zero Ecological Damage: Mandatory 50% renewable energy, zero wastewater discharge into natural streams, and 100% plastic recycling (failure triggers resort closure within 48 hours).
  • Integrated IT Townships - IITP (Reg 14.10 - Jan 2024 Amendment):
    • Threshold: Minimum contiguous land reduced to 10 Acres (4 Hectares) accessed by an 18.0-meter road.
    • FSI & Mix: 2.50 FSI in major corporations (Pune, PCMC, MMR, Nagpur), 2.00 FSI in other cities, 1.00 FSI in Agri zones. FSI is split 50% IT/ITES activities and 50% residential/commercial support, with 20% reserved for public parks.
    • Statutory Concessions: Automatic Non-Agricultural (NA) conversion, exemption from agricultural land ceilings and ULC, and staggered premium installments.
    • Infrastructure Guarantee: Developer furnishes a 15% bank guarantee of estimated infrastructure cost; must provide independent water source supplying 140 lpcd.
  • Integrated Logistics Parks - ILP (Reg 14.11):
    • ILP Scale: Minimum 5 Acres on an access road of at least 15.0 meters width. (70% logistics services, 30% support services).
    • Logistics Park (LP): Standalone warehouse building with minimum 20,000 sq.ft BUA.
    • Up to 200% Additional FSI: In addition to base industrial FSI, up to 200% additional FSI is granted at subsidized premiums: 0% (NIL) in No-Industry / Naxal districts, 10% in standard districts, and 15% in major municipal corporations (PMC, TMC, NMMC, KDMC).
    • Building Height: Permitted up to 24.0 meters to accommodate modern high-bay automated racking systems.
  • Aerospace, Defense & MIDC Areas (Reg 14.12 & 14.13):
    • Defense Townships: Deemed industrial conversion from Agri zones; up to 20% BUA for support residential/commercial.
    • R&D Incentive: Research and development facilities receive an additional +0.50 FSI bonus over permissible industrial FSI.
02 // STATUTORY RULE

Statutory Rule & Clause Breakdown

UDCPR Reg. 14.9 to 14.13 STATUTORY PROVISION

"14.9 ECO-TOURISM - Maximum permissible total built up area shall not exceed 10% of gross area with only G+1 structure having height not more than 9.0 m... Fencing permissible for only 10% in form of chain link without masonry walls... 14.10 INTEGRATED IT TOWNSHIP - Area shall not be less than 10 acres (4 Ha.)... 50% FSI for IT/ITES and 50% for residential/commercial... 14.11 INTEGRATED LOGISTIC PARK - Spread over minimum 5 acres with 15m access road... Upto 200% additional FSI admissible over basic FSI... height up to 24 m."

REG. 14.9 // WILDLIFE SAFETY

Chainlink & Plastic Rules

To ensure free animal migration corridors, solid compound walls are prohibited within 5km of sanctuaries. All plastic waste must be segregated and recycled on-site; failure mandates statutory resort closure within 48 hours.

REG. 14.10 // JAN 2024 AMENDMENT

10-Acre IITP Threshold

The Jan 2024 Gazette reduced the minimum IITP threshold from 25 acres to 10 acres (4 Hectares), allowing boutique high-density IT campuses with 2.50 FSI, deemed NA status, and independent 140 lpcd water reservoirs.

REG. 14.11.6 // +200% INDUSTRIAL FSI

High-Bay Logistics Hubs

Integrated Logistics Parks enjoy up to 200% additional FSI over base industrial limits with ceiling heights up to 24.0m, unlocking high-density vertical fulfillment centers for global e-commerce and container freight terminals.

04 // WORKED PRACTICAL EXAMPLE

Worked Practical Calculation

Scenario: High-Bay Integrated Logistics Park (ILP) Potential in MMR Peripheral Belt

A logistics developer acquires a 10-Acre contiguous land parcel (40,468 sq.m.) in an Industrial Zone in Panvel Municipal Corporation fronting an 18.0-meter road. The basic permissible industrial FSI is 1.00. The developer applies for the maximum 200% additional FSI under Regulation 14.11.6. The ASR land rate is ₹12,000 / sq.m. Calculate the permissible Built-Up Area, logistics vs support split, premium payable, and height envelope.

1. Land Area & Road Access Verification:
  • Gross Land = 10 Acres = 40,468 sq.m. (≥ 5 Acres min requirement per Reg 14.11.4)
  • Frontage Road = 18.0m (≥ 15.0m min requirement per Reg 14.11.4) → ELIGIBLE

2. Basic vs Additional FSI Calculation (Reg 14.11.6):
  • Basic Industrial FSI = 1.00 → Basic BUA = 40,468 × 1.00 = 40,468 sq.m.
  • Maximum Additional FSI (+200% of basic FSI) = +2.00 FSI
  • Additional BUA = 40,468 × 2.00 = 80,936 sq.m.
  • Total Permissible ILP BUA = 40,468 + 80,936 = 121,404 sq.m. (3.00 FSI)

3. Land & BUA Use Distribution (Reg 14.11.4):
  • Mandatory Logistics Services (Min 70%) = 121,404 × 70% = 84,982.8 sq.m. BUA (automated warehouses, freight depots)
  • Support & Common Facilities (Max 30%) = 121,404 × 30% = 36,421.2 sq.m. BUA (truck dorms, canteens, offices)

4. Premium Payable on Additional FSI (Reg 14.11.6 Table):
  • Panvel Municipal Corporation is in Category 3 → Premium Rate = 15% of ASR Land Rate
  • Premium per sq.m. = 15% × ₹12,000 = ₹1,800 / sq.m.
  • Total Additional BUA Loaded = 80,936 sq.m.
  • Total Statutory Premium = 80,936 sq.m. × ₹1,800 = ₹14,56,84,800 (₹14.57 Cr)

5. Height & Parking Privileges (Reg 14.11.4 & 14.11.7):
  • Permissible Warehouse Height = Up to 24.0 meters
  • Heavy truck parking bays, weigh bridges, and security cabins are 100% FSI-free!

Industrial Output: The project generates over 1.3 million sq.ft of high-clearance warehousing capacity on a previously standard industrial plot, paying a subsidized 15% land premium to the municipal exchequer.

05 // SCRUTINY WATCHOUTS

Common Pitfalls & Sanction Watchouts

Pitfall 1: Building Masonry Boundary Walls in Eco-Tourism Buffers

Under Reg 14.9.2.ii(c), solid masonry boundary walls are strictly barred within 5km of sanctuaries. Fortification is permitted around only 10% of the land and must consist exclusively of open chainlink wire to avoid blocking tiger/elephant migratory paths.

Pitfall 2: Discharging Untreated Sewage in Nature Resorts

Regulation 14.9.2.ii(e) strictly prohibits discharging any effluent into natural streams. Failure to operate zero-discharge recycling systems within 15 days of notice triggers mandatory statutory closure by the Planning Authority.

Pitfall 3: Floating FSI Between Industrial and Support Zones in Logistics Parks

Under Reg 14.11.4, "Floating of FSI shall not be permissible from the area of industrial zone to the area of support services or vice versa." Developers cannot transfer warehouse FSI to build larger hotels or commercial shopping complexes.
