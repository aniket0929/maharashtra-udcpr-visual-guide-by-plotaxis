Agricultural & Green Zones: Farmhouses & Permissible Non-Agri Uses

Learn what can legally be developed on agricultural, green, and no-development lands in Maharashtra under Regulation 4.11: strict farmhouse parameters (minimum 0.40 Ha, 0.04 FSI, max 400 sq.m, G+1), 44+ permissible non-agricultural activities, educational and hospital campuses, wayside highway amenities, and clean energy parks.

01 // EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Plain-Language Executive Summary

Contrary to popular belief, Maharashtra's Agricultural Zone (Green Zone - 1 / No Development Zone) is not completely frozen. Regulation 4.11 authorizes over 44 distinct categories of non-agricultural development to foster rural employment, education, healthcare, and infrastructure.

1. Statutory Farmhouse Rules (Reg. 4.11(ix))

A farmhouse is intended for the dwelling of the farmer and agricultural storage. UDCPR establishes rigid numerical boundaries:

  • Minimum Plot Size: Must be at least 0.40 Hectares (4,000 sq.m / ~1 Acre) in a single holding.
  • Maximum Permissible FSI: Exactly 0.04 FSI.
  • Absolute Built-Up Area Ceiling: Maximum 400 sq.m built-up area in any case, regardless of how large the agricultural parcel is.
  • Height & Floors: Only Ground + 1 upper floor with a maximum building height of 9.0 meters.
  • Quota Limit: Only one farmhouse per land holding is permitted, irrespective of the total holding size.

02 // STATUTORY RULE

Statutory Rule & Clause Breakdown

UDCPR Reg. 4.11 STATUTORY PROVISION

"4.11 AGRICULTURAL ZONE: The following uses shall be permissible in Agricultural Zone: ix) Farm houses shall be permitted subject to: a) Minimum plot area 0.4 Ha. One farm house per land holding... b) FSI shall not exceed 0.04 subject to a maximum built up area of 400 sq.m. in any case. Only ground + 1 floor structure with height not exceeding 9.0 m... xv) Buildings of educational, research and medical institutions with FSI of 1.00 on gross plot area, out of which 0.20 without payment of premium and remaining with premium at 20% of ASR... xvi) Integrated highway amenities on plots min 10,000 sq.m. abutting NH/SH or road not less than 18.0 m. width."

REG. 4.11(ix) // FARMHOUSE

0.04 FSI / 400 sqm Cap

Requires minimum 0.40 Ha parcel. Max 400 sq.m built area, G+1, 9.0m height. One farmhouse per revenue holding regardless of land size.

REG. 4.11(xv) // INSTITUTIONAL

1.00 FSI Campus Potential

Permits colleges and hospitals in green zones: 0.20 free basic FSI + 0.80 FSI on paying 20% ASR land rate premium. Mandatory 250 trees/Ha.

04 // WORKED PRACTICAL EXAMPLE

Worked Practical Calculation

Practical Scrutiny Problem: An agricultural landowner holds 60,000 sq.m (6.0 Hectares) in Pune district and wishes to build both a farmhouse and an engineering institute campus.

FARMHOUSE SCRUTINY
Calculated FSI area: $60,000 \times 0.04 = 2,400\text{ sq.m}$.
Statutory Cap under Reg. 4.11(ix)(b): 400 sq.m maximum.
The farmhouse is sanctioned for exactly 400 sq.m (G+1, 8.5m height).
COLLEGE CAMPUS SCRUTINY
The remaining 50,000 sq.m is earmarked for an educational institute under Reg. 4.11(xv).
Permissible FSI = 1.00 → 50,000 sq.m BUA.
• Free Basic FSI: $50,000 \times 0.20 = 10,000\text{ sq.m}$.
• Premium FSI: $50,000 \times 0.80 = 40,000\text{ sq.m}$ payable @ 20% of ASR land rate.
• Mandatory Tree Plantation: $5.0\text{ Ha} \times 250 = \mathbf{1,250\text{ trees}}$ planted on site.
✓ STATUTORY SANCTION: Farmhouse approved at 400 sqm ceiling and college campus approved at 1.00 FSI.
05 // SCRUTINY WATCHOUTS

Common Pitfalls & Sanction Watchouts

Pitfall 1: Attempting to Subdivide Agricultural Land into 0.40 Ha Farmhouse Plots

Under Maharashtra Land Revenue Code (MLRC) and UDCPR Reg. 4.11(ix)(a), only one farmhouse per land holding is permitted. Subdividing a large agricultural parcel into multiple small plots for speculative farmhouse sales violates fragmentation laws unless approved as an authorized layout under Chapter 3/5.

Pitfall 2: Counting Solar Panel Ground Footprint Toward FSI

Regulation 4.11(xx) clearly states that area covered under solar panels shall not be counted in FSI. Charging premium or restricting ground coverage for solar structures is a frequent scrutiny error by rural gram panchayat desks.

06 // AMENDMENT HISTORY

Amendment & Clarification History (#)

Corrigendum CR.121/21 (02 Dec 2021) Farmhouse 400 sq.m Ceiling Substituted

Substituted clause (ix)(b) to explicitly cap farmhouse built-up area to 400 sq.m regardless of holding size, preventing massive luxury estates from bypassing green zone restrictions under the guise of farm dwellings.
