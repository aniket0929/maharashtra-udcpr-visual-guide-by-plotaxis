Reg. 5.5 & 5.9 Amended (#) CR.83/19 & CR.121/21 Core Ecological Planning

Western Ghats, Hill Stations & High Altitude Zones (>1000m MSL)

Master the stringent ecological controls governing Maharashtra's mountainous Western Ghats (UNESCO World Heritage corridor): the 2.5 km aerial buffer around Mahabaleshwar-Panchgani, Sahyadri Tiger Project regulations, high-altitude development controls above 1,000 meters Mean Sea Level (Appendix O), and Pune Sector-R hill station standards (Lonavala, Karla, Malavali) requiring minimum 500 sq.m plots.

01 // EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Plain-Language Executive Summary

The Sahyadri mountain range (Western Ghats) contains some of the highest rainfall and richest biodiversity hotspots on Earth. To protect these fragile montane ecosystems from unplanned concrete sprawl, Regulations 5.5 and 5.9 enforce specialized high-altitude and eco-sensitive development standards.

1. Mahabaleshwar-Panchgani 2.5 km Buffer Zone (Reg. 5.5.1)

Surrounding the municipal limits of the Mahabaleshwar and Panchgani hill stations, a statutory 2.5 km aerial buffer zone is enforced:

  • Permissible activities strictly follow the MoEFCC Eco-Sensitive Zone Notification (27/02/2017).
  • Industrial, quarrying, and commercial mining operations are 100% prohibited.
  • High-rise construction is banned; buildings are restricted to low-profile ground + 1 structures designed to blend with natural contours.

2. High Altitude Regulations: Lands Above 1,000m MSL (Reg. 5.5.3 & Appendix O)

Altitude Trigger: For all lands situated above 1,000 meters of Mean Sea Level (MSL) in the Satara region:

  • Governed by specialized Appendix-O regulations designed to prevent ridge-cutting and soil erosion.
  • All development must adhere to the Buffer Zone rules of the Conservation Zone.
  • Building heights are strictly capped, and extensive terracing or tree felling is prohibited.

3. Sahyadri Tiger Reserve & Community Nature Conservancies (CNC)

  • Tiger Project Core & Buffer: Development within the Sahyadri Tiger Project is governed directly by the National Tiger Conservation Authority (NTCA) approved Tiger Conservation Plan.
  • Community Nature Conservancy (CNC): Privately owned lands situated within 5 km from wildlife sanctuary boundaries may develop low-density eco-tourism and nature homestays under strict environmental conditions.

4. Pune Sector-R: Lonavala, Karla & Malavali (Reg. 5.9.1)

  • Minimum Plot Size: In Sector-R growth centres (Lonavala–Karla–Malavali and Kune–Pangaloli–Kurwande), no plot shall be less than 500 sq.m!
  • Roof Design: Sloping roofs (minimum slope 1:3) with earthen red/terracotta finishes are mandatory to shed heavy monsoon downpours and preserve hill character.
02 // STATUTORY RULE

Statutory Rule & Clause Breakdown

UDCPR Reg. 5.5 & 5.9 STATUTORY PROVISION

"5.5 FOR SATARA REGIONAL PLAN: 5.5.1 Buffer Zone up to 2.5 km. aerial distance around Mahabaleshwar Panchgani Eco-Sensitive Zone... Users permissible shall be as per MOEF Notification dt.27/02/2017... 5.5.3 Regulations for Areas Situated Above 1000 m. of Mean Sea Level in Satara Region... Appendix O... 5.9 FOR PUNE REGIONAL PLAN: 5.9.1 Regulations for planning areas of growth centres at Sector - R (Lonavala - Karla - Malavali): No plots in these zones shall be less than 500 sq.m."

REG. 5.5.1 // 2.5 KM BUFFER

Mahabaleshwar Protection

Enforces a 2.5 km aerial ring around Mahabaleshwar-Panchgani hill stations, prohibiting high-density commercial developments and polluting uses.

REG. 5.9.1 // 500 SQ.M MIN PLOT

Lonavala / Karla Enclave

Prohibits micro-plotted subdivisions in Pune hill sectors. Every plotted bungalow site must be at least 500 sq.m to guarantee low density.

04 // WORKED PRACTICAL EXAMPLE

Worked Practical Calculation

Practical Scrutiny Problem: An architect is retained to subdivide an agricultural parcel of 8,000 sq.m in Karla (Pune Sector-R) into residential villa plots.

PLOT SIZING AUDIT
Under Regulation 5.9.1(i): "No plots in these zones shall be less than 500 sq.m."
Can the architect create 200 sq.m or 300 sq.m row-house plots?
No. Every individual plot must be at least 500 sq.m.
After leaving 15% for layout roads, net plotted area $\approx 6,800\text{ sq.m}$.
Maximum permissible plots = $\lfloor 6,800 / 500 \rfloor = \mathbf{13\text{ villa plots}}$.
ARCHITECTURAL DESIGN
Every villa is designed with a sloping terracotta roof (slope 1:2.5) and a maximum building height of 9.0 meters (Ground + 1), complying with Western Ghats hill aesthetics.
✓ STATUTORY COMPLIANCE: Layout complies with the 500 sq.m plot minimum and sloping roof mandate.
05 // SCRUTINY WATCHOUTS

Common Pitfalls & Sanction Watchouts

Pitfall 1: Designing Flat Slab Terraces in Sector-R Hill Areas

Municipal and regional branch offices in Lonavala and Karla strictly enforce sloping roof covenants. Designing flat RCC terrace roofs without pitched covers violates Regulation 5.9.1 and will be returned with objections.

Pitfall 2: Ignoring Mean Sea Level (MSL) Elevation Contours

In Satara district, once a site crosses the 1,000-meter contour line, Appendix O automatically takes effect. Submitting plans under standard Chapter 6 FSI rules without checking MSL elevation results in plan invalidation.
