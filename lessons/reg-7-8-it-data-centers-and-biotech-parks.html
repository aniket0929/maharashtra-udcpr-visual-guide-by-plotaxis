IT Establishments, Data Centers & Biotech Parks

How Maharashtra attracts global tech giants and hyperscale cloud infrastructure: mastering Regulation 7.8 (amended Jan 2024), tiered IT FSI up to 4.00, 40% commercial/residential support allowances, and zero amenity space requirements.

01 // EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Plain-Language Executive Summary

Maharashtra is India's leading destination for cloud computing, software export, and data centers. To sustain this momentum, the State Government gazetted a major overhaul of Regulation 7.8 on 12th January 2024, harmonizing UDCPR with the Maharashtra Information Technology & ITES Policy - 2023.

Under this modern framework, registered IT/ITES Parks, AVGC (Animation/VFX/Gaming) Parks, and Hyperscale Data Centers in MMR and PMR are granted soaring FSI multipliers:

• 12.0m Road Width: FSI up to 3.00
• 18.0m Road Width: FSI up to 3.50
• 27.0m Road Width: FSI up to 4.00
• Concessional Premium: Strictly 10% of ASR land rate (25% in CBD)

To create lively 24/7 "live-work-play" ecosystems, developers may utilize up to 40% of total built-up area for allied support services—including retail convenience, fine dining, recreation, and residential employee housing! Furthermore, under Regulation 7.8.1(vii), IT parks and data centers are 100% exempt from surrendering Amenity Space.

However, statutory compliance is fiercely guarded: If built space is illegally diverted to unauthorized commercial uses, a joint inspection team imposes a crushing per-day penalty of 0.3% of the prevailing ASR value until the misuse is rectified!

HIGH-DENSITY TECH

FSI Up to 4.00 on 27m Roads

IT establishments and hyperscale cloud data centers can consume an FSI of 3.00 on 12m roads, 3.50 on 18m roads, and 4.00 on 27m roads, with full eligibility for ancillary area FSI and installment facilities at 8.5% interest.

CONCESSIONAL PREMIUM

10% ASR Land Tariff

Additional FSI costs only 10% of unguided ASR (25% in CBD). The premium is deposited into a dedicated Critical Infrastructure Fund for IT/ITES and matched by Government/MIDC grants for local off-site infrastructure.

MIXED-USE ECOSYSTEM

40% Support Services Quota

To prevent isolated monofunctional enclaves, up to 40% of total BUA (50% outside MMR/PMR) can be developed as retail shopping, cafes, banks, and executive residential apartments for park employees.

PUNITIVE RECOVERY

0.3% / Day Misuse Penalty

Diverting IT space into unauthorized retail or commercial offices triggers a statutory daily penalty equal to 0.3% of the prevailing ASR capital value of the misused area, enforced jointly by Industries and Town Planning.

04 // WORKED PRACTICAL EXAMPLE

Worked Practical Calculation

Worked Case: Designing a Hyperscale Cloud Data Center Campus

Campus Profile:

  • Location: Navi Mumbai / Thane (MMR Municipal Corporation).
  • Plot Area = 20,000 sq.m (2.0 Hectares) fronting a 30.0-meter DP road.
  • User: Hyperscale Cloud Data Center & AI Computing Campus.
  • ASR Land Rate = Rs 35,000 / sq.m.

Step 1: Permissible Built-Up Potential (Reg 7.8.1)

Because road width is $30.0\text{m} \ge 27.0\text{m}$, permissible FSI = 4.00: $$\text{Total Campus BUA} = 20,000\text{ sq.m} \times 4.00 = \mathbf{80,000\text{ sq.m}}$$ $$\text{Basic FSI (1.10)} = 20,000 \times 1.10 = \mathbf{22,000\text{ sq.m}}$$ $$\text{Additional IT / Data Center FSI} = 80,000 - 22,000 = \mathbf{58,000\text{ sq.m}}$$

Step 2: Concessional Premium Valuation (10% ASR)

Under Reg 7.8.1(i), premium is strictly 10% of ASR: $$\text{Premium Rate per sq.m} = \text{Rs } 35,000 \times 10\% = \mathbf{\text{Rs } 3,500 / \text{sq.m}}$$ $$\text{Total Premium} = 58,000\text{ sq.m} \times \text{Rs } 3,500 = \mathbf{\text{Rs } 203,000,000\text{ (Rs 20.30 Crores)}}$$

Paid in 4 installments at 8.5% interest, with 100% credited to the Critical IT Infrastructure Fund.

Step 3: Support Services & Amenity Space Audits

• Amenity Space Surrender: Under Reg 7.8.1(vii), zero amenity space is surrendered (saving 1,000 sq.m of valuable land!).

• Allied Services Quota (40%): Up to $80,000 \times 40\% = \mathbf{32,000\text{ sq.m}}$ can be utilized for tech-worker residential dorms, cafeteria, gym, and client lounges.

• Incubation Quota (2%): $80,000 \times 2\% = \mathbf{1,600\text{ sq.m}}$ reserved for AI/tech startups.

05 // SCRUTINY WATCHOUTS

Common Pitfalls & Sanction Watchouts

SEVERE RISKS & PENALTIES IN IT & DATA CENTER PLANNING

  • Misusing IT Space for General Commercial Offices: Availing 10% premium IT FSI and leasing space to retail banking, law firms, or non-IT trading triggers a 0.3% per day penalty on prevailing ASR! Over 6 months, this penalty can exceed the entire capital value of the building.
  • Exceeding 40% Support Services: The 40% cap on residential and commercial support activities is strictly enforced. If support services exceed 40%, the excess area is penalized.
  • Omitting the 2% Startup Incubation Space: Reg 7.8.1(iii) mandates that at least 2% of total proposed BUA must be dedicated to incubation facilities for new units.
  • Failing to Update the Directorate of Industries Portal: Every IT park and data center must submit an annual return on the Directorate portal listing unit tenants, power consumption, and employee headcount.
