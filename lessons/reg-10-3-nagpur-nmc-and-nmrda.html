Nagpur Municipal Corporation (NMC) & NMRDA Special Schemes

As the secondary capital of Maharashtra and the central logistics crossroad of India, Nagpur operates under specialized planning regulations split across the urban core (Nagpur Municipal Corporation - NMC) and the sprawling regional belt (Nagpur Metropolitan Region Development Authority - NMRDA). Regulations 10.3 and 10.4 unlock enhanced commercial and industrial basic FSI (up to 2.50 for pure commercial and non-congested industrial), codify Industrial-to-Residential (I-to-R) conversion premiums on NIT/NMC leaseholds (15% residential / 20% commercial), sanction a 250 m residential development ribbon along the 60 m Outer Ring Road, establish land-sharing mechanics for historic NIT Improvement Schemes, and permit large-scale 15-hectare residential townships in the Agricultural Zone with 10% free land surrender.

01 // EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Plain-Language Executive Summary

Nagpur’s statutory framework reconciles historical Nagpur Improvement Trust (NIT) leasehold tenures with modern metropolitan infrastructure corridors:

  • NMC Commercial & Industrial FSI Matrix (Reg 10.3.1 & 10.3.2):
    • Commercial Zone (Non-Congested): Base FSI is 2.00 for mixed/residential use, and rises to 2.50 for purely commercial use.
    • Commercial Zone (Congested): Base FSI is 1.50 on roads < 9.0 m, and 2.00 on roads ≥ 9.0 m.
    • Industrial Zone: Base FSI is 2.50 in non-congested areas (1.00 in congested areas).
    • Large Residential Plots (Reg 10.3.4): Plots ≥ 1,000 sq.m enjoy a basic FSI of 1.25 across both congested and non-congested areas, irrespective of road width.
  • Industrial-to-Residential (I-to-R) Conversions (Reg 10.3.3):
    • Private Freehold Plots: Permitted for residential use at standard residential FSI upon payment of a 15% ASR premium on developed land rate.
    • NIT / NMC Leased Plots: FSI of 2.50 for pure commercial (premium @ 20% ASR) and 2.00 for mixed use (premium @ 15% ASR). Existing approved layouts with amenities are exempt from additional amenity surrender.
  • NMRDA 60 m Outer Ring Road Corridor (Reg 10.4.1): A designated 250-meter residential corridor along the 60 m wide Outer Ring Road is pre-sanctioned for development upon payment of government premium on the gross land area.
  • NIT Improvement Schemes Mechanics (Reg 10.4.2): On final plots under sanctioned Improvement Schemes, owners can develop with full permissible FSI of the entire original plot. Inclusive housing mandates are exempted if the Authority's share is utilized for affordable housing.
  • Mega-Townships in NMRDA Agricultural Zone (Reg 10.4.3): Residential use is permitted on farmland under strict conditions:
    • Minimum contiguous holding: 15.0 Hectares (37 acres) fronting a minimum 12.0 m road.
    • 10% Free Land Handover: 10% of gross holding handed over free to NMRDA without FSI/TDR.
    • Premium: 5% of ASR land rate paid to NMRDA. Zero-discharge STP and internal infrastructure developed by owner.
  • Gaothan Periphery Extensions in NMRDA (Reg 10.4.4): Residential expansion around village Gaothans on payment of 15% ASR premium:
    • Villages up to 5,000 population: Allowed up to 750 meters from Gaothan boundary.
    • Villages above 5,000 population: Allowed up to 1,000 meters from Gaothan boundary.

Statutory Rule & Clause Breakdown

UDCPR Reg. 10.3 & 10.4 STATUTORY PROVISION

"10.3 NAGPUR MUNICIPAL CORPORATION 10.3.1 Buildings in Commercial Zone : (a) In non-congested area : FSI for buildings outside congested area in commercial zone - the basic FSI permissible shall be 2.00 for commercial cum residential use or purely residential use and 2.50 for purely commercial use. (b) In congested area : The permissible FSI for commercial use shall be 1.50 for the plots fronting on the road having width less than 9.0 m. and 2.00 for the plots fronting on the road having width 9.0 m. or more. 10.3.2 Buildings in Industrial Zone: a) In congested area, the permissible FSI for industrial use shall be 1.00. b) In non-congested area, the permissible FSI for industrial use shall be 2.5. 10.3.3 I to R provisions - Allowing Residential / Commercial uses in Industrial Zone: Any open land or lands of closed Industrial unit... subject to payment of premium to be paid equal to 15% of the rate of developed land as given in the ASR. However, for the Industrial Plots leased out by Planning Authority (NIT / NMC)... FSI of 2.5 for purely commercial use and 2.00 for Mix use shall be permissible subject to payment of premium at the rate of 15% for Residential and 20% for Commercial use. 10.4 NAGPUR METROPOLITAN REGION DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY 10.4.1 Development along Ring Road : 250 m. Residential Zone / Residential Belt proposed along the 60 m. wide Outer Ring Road as a corridor development is sanctioned, subject to payment of premium... 10.4.3 Agriculture zone, Residential use shall be allowed subject to following conditions - a) Minimum area of land shall be 15.0 hect. b) Land shall front on minimum 12.0 m. wide existing road. e) 10% of the entire holding area shall be handed over to the Authority free of cost, without any FSI / TDR... f) Premium on gross area at the rate of 5% of land rate in ASR... 10.4.4 Development permission around the periphery of Gaothan boundary: - Up to 5000 population: 750 m. from Gaothan boundary - Above 5000 population: 1000 m. from Gaothan boundary On payment of premium calculated considering 15% rate of the said land as prescribed in the ASR."

01

NMC High Commercial & Industrial FSI (Reg 10.3)

Non-congested commercial grants 2.50 Base FSI for purely commercial and 2.00 Base FSI for mixed-use. Industrial zones achieve 2.50 Base FSI outside congested boundaries.

02

NIT Leased Industrial I-to-R (Reg 10.3.3)

Unlocks prime leaseholds: 2.50 FSI for commercial (20% ASR premium) and 2.00 FSI for mixed (15% ASR premium). Approved layouts with prior amenities are exempt from new amenity surrenders.

03

NMRDA 60m Outer Ring Corridor (Reg 10.4.1)

A continuous 250-meter residential ribbon along the 60 m Outer Ring Road is legally sanctioned for residential development upon payment of government premium on gross site area.

04

NMRDA 15-Ha Townships & Gaothans (Reg 10.4.3–4)

Permits residential townships in Agriculture zones on ≥ 15.0 Hectares with 10% free land handover. Village Gaothan periphery permits expansion up to 750m / 1,000m at 15% ASR premium.

04 // WORKED PRACTICAL EXAMPLE

Worked Practical Calculation

PRACTICAL SCRUTINY CALCULATION: NIT LEASED CONVERSION & TOWNSHIP HANDOVER

Scenario 1: NIT Leased Industrial Plot Conversion

A logistics company holds a 5,000 sq.m plot in Nagpur leased from NIT. They propose a pure commercial shopping mall. What is the built-up area and conversion premium?

Step 1: Check Permissible FSI under Reg 10.3.3
  • For NIT leased industrial plots converted to pure commercial use: Permissible FSI = 2.50.
  • Admissible Built-Up Area: $5,000\text{ sq.m} \times 2.50 = \mathbf{12,500\text{ sq.m}}$.
Step 2: Premium Calculation
  • Premium rate for pure commercial = 20% of developed land rate in current ASR.
  • If ASR developed rate is Rs. 20,000/sq.m: Premium per sq.m = $20\% \times 20,000 = \text{Rs. 4,000}$. Total Premium = $5,000 \times 4,000 = \mathbf{\text{Rs. 2.00 Crores}}$ paid to NIT/NMC.

Scenario 2: NMRDA 20-Hectare Township Land Surrender (Reg 10.4.3)

Step 3: Land Handover Computation
  • Gross holding: $20.0\text{ Hectares} \ge 15.0\text{ Ha}$ minimum fronting a 12.0 m road. Fully eligible.
  • Mandatory Surrender (Reg 10.4.3.e): 10% of entire holding area handed over to NMRDA free of cost without any FSI or TDR credit.
  • Surrender Land: $20.0\text{ Ha} \times 10\% = \mathbf{2.0\text{ Hectares (20,000 sq.m)}}$ road-fronting land transferred to NMRDA.
  • Government Premium: $5\% \times \text{ASR land rate} \times 20.0\text{ Ha}$.
05 // SCRUTINY WATCHOUTS

Common Pitfalls & Sanction Watchouts

COMMON SANCTION & SCRUTINY PITFALLS IN NAGPUR & NMRDA

  • Demanding Double Amenity Space on NIT Layouts: Requiring new amenity space when converting an industrial plot to residential/commercial. Reg 10.3.3(ii) clarifies that if amenity space was already surrendered in the approved industrial layout, no new amenity space can be insisted upon.
  • Applying Gaothan 1,000m Expansion to Small Villages: Assuming every rural village enjoys 1,000 m peripheral development. Villages with population ≤ 5,000 are restricted to 750 meters from the Gaothan boundary.
  • Claiming TDR Credit for 10% Township Land Surrender: In NMRDA 15-hectare agricultural townships, developers cannot claim TDR or FSI for the 10% land surrendered to the Authority—it is strictly free of cost and uncredited.
  • Miscalculating I-to-R Commercial Premium: Paying 15% instead of 20% on NIT leased plots. Mixed use attracts 15%, but pure commercial requires 20% of the developed ASR rate.
