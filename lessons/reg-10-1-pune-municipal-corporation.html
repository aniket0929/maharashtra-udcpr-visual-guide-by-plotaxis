Pune City Municipal Corporation: Koregaon Park Sanad, Heights & Defense Zones

Regulation 10.1 establishes specific statutory modifications for Pune Municipal Corporation (PMC) that override general state-wide provisions. It enforces elevated road-width prerequisites for high-rises (12 m road for buildings > 36 m, 15 m road for buildings >= 50 m), codifies verbatim the historical British-era Sanad Building Rules for Koregaon Park (preserving low-density bungalow typology, 20 ft road setbacks, maximum 1/3 ground coverage, and a strict G+1 storey ceiling), caps heights in ecologically sensitive Parvati and Chatushrungi view corridors at 21 m, and enforces strict security non-buildable buffers around defense installations (75 m for ARDE Pashan, and 457.2 m / 500 yards for HEMRL Sutarwadi).

01 // EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Plain-Language Executive Summary

While Chapters 1 through 9 provide standard development controls across Maharashtra, Chapter 10 recognizes unique historical, geographic, and defense realities of individual municipal corporations. For Pune (PMC), Regulation 10.1 institutes crucial overrides that every practicing architect and developer must navigate:

  • High-Rise Road Width Prerequisites (Reg 10.1.1): For tall buildings within PMC limits, heightened accessibility standards apply:
    • Height > 36.0 m: Mandatory minimum abutting road width of 12.0 m.
    • Height ≥ 50.0 m: Mandatory minimum abutting road width of 15.0 m.
  • Koregaon Park Special Sanad Rules (Reg 10.1.2): An elite historical residential precinct bounded by Koregaon Park North Road, Ghorpadi boundary, Southern Railway line, and Circuit House-Fitzgerald Bridge Road. Governed by Collector's special rules:
    • No Sub-division: Plots shown in the sanctioned layout can never be subdivided.
    • Low-Rise Cap: Maximum two storeys including ground floor (G+1). No multi-family apartment blocks exceeding two self-contained flats per floor.
    • Ground Coverage: Maximum one-third (33.33%) of the total plot area may be built upon.
    • Imperial Setbacks: Front setback 20 feet (6.10 m) on roads ≥ 40 ft; 15 feet (4.57 m) on other roads. Side margins 10 feet (3.05 m); rear margin 20 feet (6.10 m).
    • Tenement & Room Minimums: Minimum tenement area 250 sq.ft. Habitable room minimum 120 sq.ft with no side less than 10 ft. Window openings must be at least 1/7th of floor area.
  • View Corridor Height Caps (Reg 10.1.3): Strict 21.0 m height cap in Parvati (Sinhagad Road / P.L. Deshpande Udyan boundary) and Chatushrungi / Bhamburda (Ganeshkhind / Senapati Bapat Road) to preserve natural ridgelines and hill vistas.
  • Defense Prohibited Envelopes (Reg 10.1.4): Total prohibition of building construction within:
    • 75.0 meters from the crest of the outer parapet of ARDE (Armament Research and Development Establishment), Pashan Range.
    • 457.2 meters (500 yards) from the crest of the outer parapet of HEMRL (High Energy Materials Research Laboratory), Sutarwadi.
02 // STATUTORY RULE

Statutory Rule & Clause Breakdown

UDCPR Reg. 10.1 STATUTORY PROVISION

"10.1 PUNE CITY MUNICIPAL CORPORATION 10.1.1 Height of Building: For the building having height more than 36 m., the minimum road width shall be 12 m. and for building having height equal to or more than 50 m., the minimum road width shall be 15 m. 10.1.2 The height restriction in Koregaon Park Area shall be as per special regulations applicable in Koregaon Park Area: b) Special Building Rules framed by the Collector of Pune for this area: - iv) Every building shall be set back at least 20 feet from the boundary of any road, 40 feet or more in width and 15 feet from other roads. - v) No building shall be constructed within 10 feet of side boundaries. - vi) Distance between main building and rear boundary: not less than 20 feet. - viii) Not more than one-third of the total area of any building plot shall be built upon. - ix) No building shall contain more than two storeys including the ground floor. - x) If flats are constructed there shall be not more than two self-contained flats on each floor. 10.1.3 Notwithstanding anything contained in these regulations, height of building shall not be more than 21.0 m. in: a) Parvati: Area bounded by Singhagad Road on north and west, P.L. Deshpande Udyan on south... b) Chatushrungi: S.No.105, 106 pt., 107 pt. of village Bhamburda... 10.1.4 No piece of land shall be used as site for construction of building: (a) If within prohibited distance (currently 75 m.) from crest of outer parapet of ARDE, Pashan; (b) If within prohibited distance of approximately 457.2 m. (500 yards) from crest of outer parapet of HEMRL, Sutarwadi."

01

PMC High-Rise Road Thresholds (Reg 10.1.1)

Buildings taller than 36.0 m mandate a minimum abutting road width of 12.0 m. Buildings reaching or exceeding 50.0 m mandate an abutting road width of 15.0 m, overriding standard 9.0 m baseline limits.

02

Koregaon Park Special Sanad Rules (Reg 10.1.2)

Original British-era Collector Sanad covenants remain legally binding: Max G+1 storey height, Max 1/3 (33.33%) ground coverage, 20 ft front/rear setbacks, 10 ft side margins, and zero plot subdivision permitted.

03

Parvati & Chatushrungi View Caps (Reg 10.1.3)

To safeguard visual sightlines of historic hilltops and temple shrines, building heights in demarcated sectors of Parvati (Sinhagad Road) and Chatushrungi / Bhamburda are strictly capped at 21.0 meters.

04

Military & High Explosive Buffers (Reg 10.1.4)

Complete prohibition on building construction within 75.0 m of ARDE Range Pashan, and within 457.2 m (500 yards) from the outer parapet of HEMRL Sutarwadi, as marked on the sanctioned Pune DP.

04 // WORKED PRACTICAL EXAMPLE

Worked Practical Calculation

PRACTICAL SCRUTINY CALCULATION: HIGH-RISE & BUNGALOW FEASIBILITY IN PUNE

Scenario 1: High-Rise Road Feasibility in Baner (PMC)

A developer holds a 4,000 sq.m plot in Baner fronting an existing 9.0 m wide road, which is marked with a 12.0 m DP road widening line. The developer proposes a 45.0 m tall residential tower. Is this proposal sanctionable?

Step 1: Scrutinize Height Threshold under Reg 10.1.1
  • Proposed height: $45.0\text{ m}$.
  • Regulation 10.1.1 mandates: For heights $> 36.0\text{ m}$, the minimum abutting road width must be 12.0 m.
  • Existing physical road width: $9.0\text{ m}$.
Step 2: Legal Status of DP Road Widening
  • Under statutory planning rules, road width is evaluated based on the actual developed road width available on site before grant of commencement / occupation.
  • If the road is not widened to a physical $12.0\text{ m}$, the building height is strictly capped at $36.0\text{ m}$ on the $9.0\text{ m}$ road.

Scenario 2: Koregaon Park Coverage & Storey Verification

An owner of a 20,000 sq.ft plot in Koregaon Park submits plans for a G+4 apartment building with 50% ground coverage (10,000 sq.ft footprint).

Step 3: Verification under Collector's Sanad Rules (Reg 10.1.2)
  • Storey Restriction (Clause ix): No building shall exceed two storeys including ground floor (G+1). The proposed G+4 is rejected.
  • Ground Coverage (Clause viii): Max built-upon area is $1/3\text{rd} = 20,000 / 3 = \mathbf{6,666.67\text{ sq.ft}}$. The proposed 10,000 sq.ft footprint is illegal.
  • Tenement Cap (Clause x): Max 2 self-contained flats per floor. Multi-family apartment towers are barred.
05 // SCRUTINY WATCHOUTS

Common Pitfalls & Sanction Watchouts

COMMON SANCTION & SCRUTINY PITFALLS IN PUNE (PMC)

  • Subdividing Koregaon Park Plots: Submitting layout subdivision plans for large Koregaon Park plots. Reg 10.1.2(b)(i) strictly decrees: "No building plot as shown in the layout shall be sub-divided."
  • Ignoring HEMRL 457.2 m Prohibited Distance: Purchasing or sanctioning land within 500 yards (457.2 m) of HEMRL Sutarwadi. Building proposals in this zone cannot be regularized or approved without central MoD defense de-notification.
  • Exceeding 21 m in Parvati & Chatushrungi: Assuming standard Table 6-A height calculations apply near Sinhagad Road or Senapati Bapat Road hills. Reg 10.1.3 imposes a hard statutory ceiling of 21.0 m.
  • Metric vs Imperial Discrepancies in Koregaon Park: Preparing plans in metric meters without respecting the 20 ft (6.10 m) front/rear and 10 ft (3.05 m) side setbacks established under the original imperial Sanad.
06 // AMENDMENT HISTORY

Amendment & Clarification History (#)

Statutory History & Clarifications for Pune

Proposed Sunset Clause & Stay (Notice No. CR 42/2023/UD-13 dt. 04-01-2024 & Stay dt. 03-04-2024):

A modification was proposed to cease operation of Reg 10.1.1 under Section 37(1AA). However, the Urban Development Department (UDD) issued a formal stay on 03-04-2024, ensuring that the heightened road width requirements (12 m for > 36 m, 15 m for >= 50 m) remain in full active force for PMC.
