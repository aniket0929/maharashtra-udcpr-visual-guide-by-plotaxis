Higher FSI for Institutional, Medical & Special Uses (Table 7-A)

How public policy supercharges essential civic infrastructure: understanding Table 7-A entitlements that unlock maximum building potential for educational campuses, hospitals, and starred tourism units at heavily discounted premium tariffs.

01 // EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Plain-Language Executive Summary

While Chapter 6 establishes the baseline commercial and residential building potential, Chapter 7 represents Maharashtra's strategic urban policy toolkit. Under Regulation 7.1 (Table 7-A), the State grants massive Higher Floor Space Index (Higher FSI) to critical civic, social, and economic functions—including schools, universities, medical hospitals, star-category hotels, religious buildings, and government institutions.

For these qualifying uses, the permissible higher FSI equals the Maximum Building Potential of the fronting road (from Table 6-A or Table 6-G) minus the Basic FSI. Instead of paying the standard 35% ASR premium charged to commercial developers, qualifying institutions pay heavily discounted concessional tariffs:

• Pre-primary & Primary Schools: 5% of ASR land rate
• Charitable Hospitals & Educational Trusts: 10% of ASR land rate
• Private Hospitals & Higher Education: 15% of ASR land rate
• 2-Star & Above Hotels / Tourism Projects: 20% of ASR land rate (with 1-step road width bonus!)

Furthermore, under Regulation 7.0(h), projects developed under Table 7-A are 100% exempt from surrendering Amenity Space under Regulation 3.5! In agricultural zones, schools and hospitals are entitled to 100% additional FSI over and above the agricultural base.

02 // STATUTORY RULE

Statutory Rule & Clause Breakdown

UDCPR Regulation 7.0 & 7.1 STATUTORY PROVISION

" ### 7.0 GENERAL Higher Floor Space Index may be allowed for certain uses in congested and non-congested area, except as otherwise specified and subject to following conditions : i) Permissible higher FSI for the buildings as mentioned in Table No.7-A shall be the maximum permissible building potential according to road width as mentioned in column 6, 9 of Table No.6-A or column 6, 9 of Table No.6-G under Regulation No.6.1 or 6.3 (whichever is applicable) minus Basic FSI. Instead of availing this higher FSI, the owner shall be entitled to avail premium FSI / TDR or both to that extent. iii) Premium: Premium for higher F.S.I. shall be as per column 4 of Table No.7-A... shared 50:50 between State Government & the Authority. h) No Amenity Spaces as per Regulation No.3.5 shall be required to be provided for the uses mentioned in Table No.7-A. j) In agricultural zone, uses mentioned at Sr.No.A & B of table 7-A, shall be entitled for 100% additional FSI over and above, permissible in said zone. ### Table No.7-A (Higher FSI Concessions) - Educational: i) Pre-primary, nursery, Special Educational for challenged: 5% premium. ii) Primary School: 5% premium. iii) Other Educational (colleges, hostels within 500m): 10% for Charitable; 15% for Private. - Medical Institutions: Hospitals, Maternity Homes: Max building potential considered 3.00 for roads >= 18.0 m. Premium: 10% Charitable / 15% Private. - Starred Category Hotels (2-star & above) & Large Tourism Projects: Premium: 20%. Maximum building potential allowed considering road width one step below (e.g. 12m road gets 15m potential). - Religious Buildings: Premium: 15%. Police & District Magistrate (Collector) NOC required. - Basic Shelter for EWS/LIG by public authority: FSI up to 2.5 or max potential, whichever is maximum. NO PREMIUM. - Students / Working Women Hostels (dormitories, room <= 17 sq.m.): Premium: 10%. Rental only, cannot be sold. "

CIVIC INFRASTRUCTURE

Schools & Playgrounds

Pre-primary and primary schools pay only 5% ASR premium for full road potential. Playground protection: If expanding on vacant land, the playground area must not be less than 40% of plot area or existing playground area, whichever is minimum.

HEALTHCARE EXPANSION

Hospitals Up to 3.00 FSI

Hospitals fronting roads ≥ 18.0m are automatically granted a building potential of 3.00 FSI. Registered charitable institutions pay a concessional 10% ASR premium, while private hospitals pay 15% (compared to standard 35% commercial rates).

TOURISM ACCELERATOR

Starred Hotels "One-Step" Rule

Classified 2-star and above hotels pay 20% ASR premium and receive an extraordinary planning concession: building potential is calculated one road-width step below! A hotel on a 12.0m road gets the potential of a 15.0m road ($2.50\text{ FSI}$ instead of $2.25$).

STATUTORY RELAXATION

Zero Amenity Space (Reg 7.0h)

Because educational, healthcare, and public welfare uses are themselves public amenities, developments availing Table 7-A are 100% exempt from surrendering Amenity Space under Reg 3.5, saving up to 5% of gross plot area!

04 // WORKED PRACTICAL EXAMPLE

Worked Practical Calculation

Worked Case: Higher FSI Computation for a Charitable Super-Specialty Hospital

Site Setup:

  • Plot Area = 5,000 sq.m in a Municipal Corporation on an 18.0-meter DP road.
  • User: Registered Charitable Medical Trust constructing a multi-specialty hospital.
  • ASR Land Rate = Rs 40,000 / sq.m.

Step 1: Determine FSI Entitlement under Table 7-A (Category B)

For hospitals fronting roads ≥ 18.0m, Table 7-A Category B mandates: $$\text{Permissible Maximum Building Potential} = \mathbf{3.00}$$ $$\text{Basic FSI (Table 6-G)} = 1.10$$ $$\text{Additional Higher FSI Entitlement} = 3.00 - 1.10 = \mathbf{1.90}$$ $$\text{Total Sanctionable BUA (before Ancillary)} = 5,000\text{ sq.m} \times 3.00 = \mathbf{15,000\text{ sq.m}}$$

Step 2: Calculate Concessional Premium for Charitable Trust

Standard commercial premium FSI is charged at 35% of ASR. Under Table 7-A, a Charitable Medical Trust pays only 10% of ASR! $$\text{Higher FSI BUA} = 5,000 \times 1.90 = \mathbf{9,500\text{ sq.m}}$$ $$\text{Concessional Premium Rate} = \text{Rs } 40,000 \times 10\% = \mathbf{\text{Rs } 4,000 / \text{sq.m}}$$ $$\text{Total Premium Payable} = 9,500 \times \text{Rs } 4,000 = \mathbf{\text{Rs } 38,000,000}$$

Savings: Under commercial rates (35%), premium would have been Rs 133,000,000. Table 7-A saves the hospital trust Rs 9.5 Crores!

Step 3: Amenity Space Surrender Exemption (Reg 7.0h)

Normally, a 5,000 sq.m layout requires surrendering 5% amenity space (250 sq.m). Under Reg 7.0(h), zero amenity space is required, allowing 100% of the land to be built upon.

05 // SCRUTINY WATCHOUTS

Common Pitfalls & Sanction Watchouts

STATUTORY COMPLIANCE PITFALLS IN CHAPTER 7 HIGHER FSI

  • Selling Student or Working Women Hostel Units: Table 7-A Category J grants 10% premium FSI for hostels on the strict condition that units cannot be sold. Attempting to sell rooms as studio apartments triggers criminal fraud and cancellation of development permission.
  • Reducing Existing School Playgrounds Below 40%: When adding upper floors or expanding educational buildings, you cannot shrink the playground below 40% of the plot area or the existing playground area.
  • Applying Starred Hotel "One-Step" Rule to Normal Lodges: The one-step road-width bonus is restricted exclusively to hotels possessing a valid Star classification certificate from the Ministry of Tourism, Government of India.
  • Failing to Secure District Magistrate NOC for Religious Buildings: Religious structures under Category F cannot receive building permission without prior written NOCs from both the Police Commissioner/SP and the District Collector.
