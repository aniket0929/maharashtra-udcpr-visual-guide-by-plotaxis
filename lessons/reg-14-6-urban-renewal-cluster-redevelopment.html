Urban Renewal Schemes (URS) - Cluster Redevelopment & Master Renewal

To prevent catastrophic structural collapses of aging urban cores and achieve comprehensive neighborhood revitalization, Regulation 14.8 establishes the Urban Renewal Scheme (URS), widely known as Cluster Redevelopment. Departing from piecemeal single-plot redevelopments that choke urban streets, URS amalgamates vast swathes of land (minimum 10,000 sq.m. in non-congested areas and 4,000 sq.m. in congested gaothans) accessed by an 18.0-meter road. Under Table 14-X, authorized owners receive brand-new flats (minimum 30 sq.m.) plus a mandatory 25% bonus area free of cost. The scheme unlocks global FSI of 4.00 or higher based on the ratio of Land Rate to Construction Rate (LR/RC), transforming decaying tenements into planned micro-cities complete with widened boulevards, public parks, schools, and Urban Renewal TDR (URT).

01 // EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Plain-Language Executive Summary

Urban Renewal Schemes unlock large-scale urban transformations by consolidating multiple aging plots, slums, and dilapidated chawls into an integrated masterplan under the oversight of a High Power Committee (HPC):

  • Cluster Land & Road Thresholds (Reg 14.8.1):
    • Non-Congested Areas: Minimum cluster area of 10,000 sq.m. (1.0 Ha). (Can be relaxed to 8,000 sq.m. by Municipal Commissioner & HPC for natural hardships like railway lines or nallahs).
    • Congested Gaothan Areas: Minimum cluster area of 4,000 sq.m. (also 4,000 sq.m. across Ulhasnagar).
    • External Access: Must be accessible by an existing or proposed DP road at least 18.0 meters wide.
  • Cluster Mix & Building Age Criteria (Reg 14.8.1.iii):
    • Eligible Structures: Authorized or unauthorized buildings at least 30 years of age (calculated from OC or tax assessment).
    • Dilapidated Buildings: Structures under 30 years declared structurally unsafe or unfit for human habitation by the Designated Officer.
    • Slum Component: Declared slums (pre-1995) permitted up to maximum 25% of cluster area.
    • Younger Sound Buildings (<30 yrs): Allowed up to 40% of cluster area for planning integrity; total sound structures + slums cannot exceed 50% in aggregate.
  • Rehabilitation Entitlement & Table 14-X:
    • Authorized Residential Owners: Entitled to carpet area equivalent to their old home, minimum 30 sq.m., PLUS an additional 25% bonus carpet area completely free of cost.
    • Unauthorized Residential Occupants: Free up to 30 sq.m.; area between 30 and 50 sq.m. at ASR Construction Cost; area above 50 sq.m. at Full ASR Market Rate.
    • Commercial Occupants: Actual occupied carpet area; unauthorized get free up to 16.75 sq.m., balance up to 40 sq.m. at construction cost, and above 40 sq.m. at market rate.
  • FSI Calculation & LR/RC Incentive Matrix (Reg 14.8.6):
    • Total Permissible FSI = Rehab BUA + Incentive BUA, or 4.00, whichever is higher.
    • The incentive multiplier is determined by the ratio of Land Rate (LR) to Construction Rate (RC) and cluster scale (ranging from 1.75 to 3.00).
  • Urban Renewal TDR (URT) & Inalienability:
    • FSI unconsumed on-site is converted into Urban Renewal TDR (URT) tradable across the municipal jurisdiction.
    • Rehabilitation tenements cannot be sold or transferred for 15 years (authorized units transfer free of premium; others pay 10% to 25% ASR differential premium).
02 // STATUTORY RULE

Statutory Rule & Clause Breakdown

UDCPR Reg. 14.8 (Table 14-X) STATUTORY PROVISION

"14.8 URBAN RENEWAL SCHEME - Minimum area of 10,000 sq.m. in non-congested area and 4000 sq.m. in congested area... accessible by DP road at least 18.0 m. wide... Authorized residential occupants shall be entitled to minimum 30 sq.m. carpet area and additional 25% area over and above eligible area free of cost... Permissible FSI shall be FSI required for rehabilitation + incentive FSI, or 4.00 whichever is higher... Table 14-X sets terms of allotment for unauthorized buildings and slums."

REG. 14.8.4 // 25% FREE BONUS

Authorized Owner Protection

To incentivize legal owners to join cluster renewal, the statute guarantees their original carpet area (minimum 30 sq.m.) plus an automatic 25% expansion free of cost. A 40 sqm old flat receives a brand-new 50 sqm ownership flat at zero cost.

REG. 14.8.5.V // SUMMARY EVICTION

15-Day Non-Cooperation Eviction

Dissenting unauthorized occupants who refuse to join within 15 days of scheme approval face summary eviction under MRTP Act. If they hold out until building permission is issued, they completely forfeit all rights to a new tenement.

REG. 14.8.6.II // URT RELEASE

Urban Renewal TDR Safeguards

URT release is strictly pegged to completed construction: released URT can never exceed the BUA of buildings with full OC plus 50% of BUA under construction without OC, ensuring developer delivery before liquidity exit.

04 // WORKED PRACTICAL EXAMPLE

Worked Practical Calculation

Scenario: 1.20 Hectare Congested Gaothan Cluster Redevelopment

A promoter amalgamates an Urban Renewal Cluster of 12,000 sq.m. (1.20 Hectares) in a congested area in Thane. The cluster contains 300 authorized residential units (averaging 40 sq.m. carpet area) and 100 unauthorized residential units (averaging 25 sq.m. carpet area). The road frontage is a 24.0-meter DP Road. Applicable ASR Land Rate (LR) is ₹36,000 / sq.m., and Rate of Construction (RC) is ₹20,000 / sq.m. Calculate the Rehab Component, Incentive FSI, Total Scheme FSI, and resulting URT potential.

1. Rehabilitation Entitlement Calculations:
  • Authorized Units (300 nos): 40 sqm + 25% bonus = 50 sq.m. carpet each (Free)
    Subtotal Carpet = 300 × 50 = 15,000 sq.m.
  • Unauthorized Units (100 nos): 25 sqm each (≤30 sqm is Free under Table 14-X)
    Subtotal Carpet = 100 × 25 = 2,500 sq.m.
  • Total Rehab Carpet Area = 15,000 + 2,500 = 17,500 sq.m.
  • Rehab Built-Up Area (BUA at 1.25 multiplier) = 17,500 × 1.25 = 21,875 sq.m.

2. Basic Ratio (LR / RC) & Incentive Multiplier:
  • LR / RC = ₹36,000 / ₹20,000 = 1.80
  • For LR/RC between 1.50 and 2.00 on a 1.20 Ha plot (1.0 to 5.0 Ha column):
    Incentive Multiplier = 2.25

3. Incentive Built-Up Area & Total Permissible BUA:
  • Incentive BUA = 21,875 sq.m. × 2.25 = 49,218.75 sq.m.
  • Total Permissible BUA = 21,875 + 49,218.75 = 71,093.75 sq.m.
  • Equivalent Cluster FSI on 12,000 sqm = 71,093.75 / 12,000 = 5.92 FSI (Well above the 4.00 statutory floor!)

4. On-Site Construction vs Urban Renewal TDR (URT):
  • Maximum on-site BUA consumed under height/air-funnel limit = 12,000 × 4.00 = 48,000 sq.m.
  • Rehab BUA consumed on site = 21,875 sq.m.
  • On-site Free Sale BUA = 48,000 - 21,875 = 26,125 sq.m.
  • Urban Renewal TDR (URT) Generated = 71,093.75 - 48,000 = 23,093.75 sq.m. DRC!

Socio-Economic Balance: 400 families receive brand-new, earthquake-resistant homes with gardens and wide roads, while the promoter sells 26,125 sq.m. of luxury apartments on-site and 23,093 sq.m. of URT across the city.

05 // SCRUTINY WATCHOUTS

Common Pitfalls & Sanction Watchouts

Pitfall 1: Exceeding the 25% Slum Area Cap in the Cluster

Under Reg 14.8.1.iii(g), slum pockets included in a URS cannot exceed 25% of the total cluster area. If a cluster proposal contains 35% slum land, it will be disqualified from URS and must be processed under Chapter 14.6 SRS instead.

Pitfall 2: Including Sound Buildings (<30 yrs) Exceeding 40%

Explanation-3 to Reg 14.8.1 caps structurally sound buildings under 30 years at 40% of cluster area, and mandates 70% consent from their title holders. If consent is absent, the building must be retained as-is and excluded from FSI calculations.

Pitfall 3: Failing to Grant the Mandatory 25% Bonus to Legal Owners

Under Reg 14.8.4.ii, the 25% additional carpet area for authorized owners is statutory and non-negotiable. Agreements offering only 1:1 carpet exchange violate the UDCPR and will be rejected by the High Power Committee.
