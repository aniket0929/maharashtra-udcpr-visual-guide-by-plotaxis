Reg. 10.5 & 10.9 Amended (#) Statutory UDCPR-2020 Sanction & Heritage Protection Circulars (Kolhapur Municipal Corporation) STATUTORY • NASHIK & KOLHAPUR

Nashik Riverfronts & Kolhapur Heritage Enclaves (Mahalaxmi Kiranotsav Marg)

Heritage cities possess delicate cultural fabrics and historic land-tenure patterns that cannot be governed by generic modern setbacks. Regulations 10.5 and 10.9 deliver tailored statutory protection for two of Maharashtra’s most culturally significant cities: Nashik and Kolhapur. In Nashik, it codifies dedicated non-motorized cycle tracks along the sacred Godavari River and establishes a relaxed 3.0 m front setback plate along the 36 m Gangapur–Ambad DP arterial road. In Kolhapur, it legally preserves Chhatrapati Shahu Maharaj-era princely town planning plots under Table 10-B (Rajarampuri, Shahupuri, Laxmipuri, Tarabai Park) and enforces strict Municipal Commissioner height ceilings along Kiranotsav Marg to ensure uninterrupted solar illumination of the Mahalaxmi Temple sanctum sanctorum during the bi-annual Kiranotsav festival.

01 // EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Plain-Language Executive Summary

Regulations 10.5 and 10.9 demonstrate how Maharashtra’s planning laws protect royal heritage layouts and astronomical sacred corridors:

  • Nashik River & Nallah Green Corridors (Reg 10.5.2): Dedicated non-motorized transport (NMT) cycle tracks are reserved along the Godavari River and designated nallahs. These greenways serve both as ecological buffers and urban recreational corridors.
  • Gangapur to Ambad 36.0 m DP Road (Reg 10.5.3): A tailored setback plate overrides Chapter 6 for plots abutting this crucial cross-city connector (Gangapur S.No. 12 to Ambad S.No. 199):
    • Minimum Plot: Area ≥ 300 sq.m, minimum plot frontage width of 12.0 m.
    • Front Setback: Only 3.00 meters (applicable even for commercial occupancy, where standard UDCPR requires 4.5 m to 6.0 m).
    • Side & Rear Margins: 2.00 meters each for structures up to Ground + 2 or Stilt + 3 floors.
  • Kolhapur Historic Layouts (Table 10-B / Reg 10.9.1): Kolhapur retains imperial layout standards based on royal feet-and-inch measurements:
    • Rajarampuri: Standard plots of 40' × 78' (3,120 sq.ft / 294 sq.m) observe 10.0 ft (3.05 m) front, 2.0 ft (0.60 m) rear, and 2.6 ft (0.76 m) side margins.
    • Shahupuri Commercial Lane: Intense bazaar plots (30' × 75' or 40' × 75') observe minimal 1.0 ft (0.305 m) front and rear margins and 1.06 ft to 2.6 ft side margins.
    • Laxmipuri: Large plots of 80' × 160' (12,800 sq.ft) observe 20.0 ft (6.0 m) front and 5.0 ft (1.52 m) side/rear margins.
    • Tarabai Park & Sykes Extension: Elite residential enclaves requiring 15.0 ft (4.56 m) front and 10.0 ft (3.05 m) rear/side setbacks for plots ≥ 5,000 sq.ft.
  • The Sacred Mahalaxmi Temple Kiranotsav Marg (Reg 10.9.2): Twice a year, during the ancient Kiranotsav (Festival of Sun Rays), the rays of the setting sun travel down the street corridor, enter the Mahadwar, and bathe the feet, chest, and face of the Mahalakshmi idol in gold. Under Regulation 10.9.2, the Municipal Commissioner has absolute discretion to fix and restrict the height of any building along Kiranotsav Marg to prevent the slightest shadow or obstruction of the astronomical sun path.

Nashik NMT Cycle Corridors (Reg 10.5.2)

Provisions for dedicated cycle tracks along the sacred Godavari River and DP nallahs, integrating ecological riverbank protection with green active mobility routes across Nashik.

02

Gangapur–Ambad 36m Road Relief (Reg 10.5.3)

Special setback concession on 36 m DP road: Front setback is only 3.00 m (even for commercial), with 2.00 m side and rear margins for G+2 or Stilt+3 buildings on plots ≥ 300 sq.m.

03

Kolhapur Historic Layouts (Table 10-B)

Codifies royal imperial layouts: Rajarampuri ($40'\times 78'$), Shahupuri continuous shopfronts (1.0 ft front/rear), Laxmipuri grand merchant plots ($80'\times 160'$), and Tarabai Park garden estates.

04

Mahalaxmi Kiranotsav Marg (Reg 10.9.2)

Building heights along the sacred astronomical sun-path corridor leading to the Mahalaxmi idol are strictly fixed by the Municipal Commissioner to preserve the bi-annual solar illumination event.

04 // WORKED PRACTICAL EXAMPLE

Worked Practical Calculation

PRACTICAL SCRUTINY CALCULATION: COMMERCIAL FRONTAGE IN NASHIK & KOLHAPUR VISTAS

Scenario 1: Commercial Building on Gangapur–Ambad 36m Road (Nashik)

An architect plans a G+2 commercial showroom on a 350 sq.m plot (14.0 m frontage) on the 36 m Gangapur to Ambad DP road. Standard Chapter 6 mandates a 6.0 m front setback. How much depth is gained under Reg 10.5.3?

Step 1: Check Plot & Structure Eligibility
  • Plot Area: $350\text{ sq.m} \ge 300\text{ sq.m}$ (Eligible).
  • Plot Frontage Width: $14.0\text{ m} \ge 12.0\text{ m}$ (Eligible).
  • Building Height: Ground + 2 structure (Eligible).
Step 2: Statutory Setback Calculation
  • Front Setback under Reg 10.5.3: 3.00 meters (for commercial use also).
  • Standard Table 6-A Setback: $6.00\text{ meters}$.
  • Usable Depth Gained: $6.00\text{ m} - 3.00\text{ m} = \mathbf{3.00\text{ meters}}$ across the entire 14.0 m frontage ($42\text{ sq.m}$ additional commercial ground footprint).
  • Side & Rear Margins: $2.00\text{ m}$ each.

Scenario 2: Shadow Analysis on Kolhapur Kiranotsav Marg (Reg 10.9.2)

Step 3: Solar Clearance Scrutiny
  • During Kiranotsav (Jan 31 – Feb 2 and Nov 9 – 11), setting sun rays penetrate the Mahadwar at an angle of 7° to 12° above the horizon.
  • Under Regulation 10.9.2, the Municipal Commissioner conducts a 3D solar trajectory simulation. Any proposed structure casting a shadow over the sanctum portal during the 5:00 PM to 5:45 PM window is refused permission or truncated in height.
05 // SCRUTINY WATCHOUTS

Common Pitfalls & Sanction Watchouts

COMMON SANCTION & SCRUTINY PITFALLS IN NASHIK & KOLHAPUR

  • Imposing Generic 6.0 m Margins on Gangapur Road: Scrutiny officers mistakenly applying standard Chapter 6 road-width setbacks (6m) to the Gangapur–Ambad 36m DP road, ignoring the explicit 3.0m statutory override in Reg 10.5.3.
  • Subdividing Rajarampuri Heritage Plots: Attempting to split a standard 40' x 78' plot in Rajarampuri into two smaller plots. The Municipal Corporation rejects layouts violating Table 10-B characteristic sizes.
  • Building Tall Towers on Kiranotsav Marg: Assuming that owning a large plot on Kiranotsav Marg entitles the developer to high-rise FSI potential. The Municipal Commissioner has absolute statutory power under Reg 10.9.2 to cap heights to protect the temple's solar rays.
  • Ignoring Cycle Track Reservations in Nashik: Constructing compound walls or ramps directly into the designated Godavari riverfront cycle track reserve.

Statutory History & Clarifications for Nashik & Kolhapur

Preservation of Historic Municipal Regulations:

Regulation 10.9.2 ensures ancient astronomical temple alignment is protected in statutory town planning law.
