Navi Mumbai (NMMC) & CIDCO Ecosystem: 10.10.2 Mega-Redevelopment & 12.5% GES

Navi Mumbai stands as one of the world’s largest planned urban agglomerations, conceived by CIDCO and administered jointly with NMMC. Regulations 10.10 and 10.14 govern this high-growth node with powerful statutory levers: setting a basic FSI of 1.50 for commercial/residential plots >= 1,000 sq.m on >= 15 m roads (with a special +0.50 building potential bonus), establishing the transformative Regulation 10.10.2 framework for the reconstruction and redevelopment of aging CIDCO housing (>30 years of age or dilapidated) granting FSI up to 3.00, guaranteeing existing owners an automatic 35% carpet area expansion (min 300 sq.ft) plus Table 10-D plot consolidation bonuses up to 20%, and codifying the 12.5% / 22.5% Gaothan Expansion Schemes (Land Compensation Scheme) with Pushpak Node TDR utilization.

01 // EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Plain-Language Executive Summary

Navi Mumbai’s unique master-planned layout requires distinct FSI and renewal policies to manage its aging residential condominiums:

  • Basic FSI & Building Potential (Reg 10.10.1):
    • Plots < 1,000 sq.m: Basic FSI is 1.00.
    • Plots ≥ 1,000 sq.m & fronting ≥ 15.0 m road: Basic FSI is 1.50. (Grandfathering clause: CIDCO plots previously leased with 1.50 FSI on roads < 15 m are exempt from the 15 m road width requirement).
    • Potential Bonus: On plots where basic FSI is 1.50, the maximum building potential from Table 6-G can be exceeded by an additional 0.50 FSI.
    • PAP 12.5% / 22.5% Allotments: Basic FSI is 1.50 (with up to 15% commercial use). Permissible building height is capped at 13.0 m for row houses (1.5 m front margin), semi-detached (3.0 m front), and detached structures (3.0 m all around).
  • Regulation 10.10.2 Mega-Redevelopment Scheme (Aging CIDCO Buildings): Applies to CIDCO-constructed residential complexes that are either declared dilapidated or have exceeded 30 years of age (irrespective of structural condition):
    • Permissible FSI (Table 10-C):
      • Plot ≥ 1,000 sq.m on ≥ 15.0 m road: FSI = 3.00.
      • Plot ≥ 1,000 sq.m on ≥ 9.0 m road: FSI = 2.00.
      • All other plots (road < 9.0 m): FSI = 1.80 (or authorized FSI + 50% incentive).
    • Rehabilitation Carpet Entitlement: Every existing resident is statutorily entitled to: $$\text{Rehab Carpet} = (\text{Existing Carpet} \times 1.35) + \text{Table 10-D Plot Bonus}$$ Subject to an absolute minimum of 300 sq.ft carpet area.
    • Table 10-D Plot Consolidation Bonus:
      • Plots up to 4,000 sq.m: Nil.
      • Above 4,000 sq.m to 2 Ha: +10% additional carpet area.
      • Above 2 Ha to 5 Ha: +15% additional carpet area.
      • Above 5 Ha to 10 Ha: +20% additional carpet area.
  • CIDCO Land Compensation Scheme - 12.5% GES (Reg 10.14.2): Allotment of developed plots to Project Affected Persons (PAP):
    • Component-I: 10% developed land in lieu of monetary compensation (after 30% deduction for infrastructure).
    • Component-II: 12.5% developed land (after 30% infrastructure deduction). Maximum 15% commercial use.
    • Pushpak Node TDR: TDR utilization up to 0.50 FSI permitted along service roads and roads ≥ 20.0 m.
  • Temporary Development on Notified Land (Reg 10.14.3): Land notified for acquisition but not yet acquired can obtain temporary 1-year renewable permissions upon depositing a Security Deposit of Rs. 10 per sq.m of proposed floor area.

01

NMMC Base FSI & +0.50 Bonus (Reg 10.10.1)

Plots ≥ 1,000 sq.m on roads ≥ 15.0 m achieve 1.50 Base FSI. Crucially, Note 3 allows the total building potential from Table 6-G to be exceeded by an additional 0.50 FSI.

02

10.10.2 Mega-Redevelopment Scheme

Applies to CIDCO buildings above 30 years of age or declared dilapidated. Unlocks FSI 3.00 on roads ≥ 15 m, and FSI 2.00 on roads ≥ 9 m, based on gross plot area.

03

Rehab Carpet & Cluster Bonus (Table 10-D)

Every resident receives Existing Carpet + 35% (min 300 sq.ft). Society amalgamations receive additional plot bonuses: +10% (4,000m²–2Ha), +15% (2–5Ha), and +20% (5–10Ha).

04

12.5% GES & Pushpak Node TDR (Reg 10.14)

Governs CIDCO Land Compensation Schemes (10% & 12.5% allotments) with 13 m height caps for PAP houses. Allows up to 0.50 TDR loading in Pushpak Node on roads ≥ 20.0 m.

04 // WORKED PRACTICAL EXAMPLE

Worked Practical Calculation

PRACTICAL SCRUTINY CALCULATION: CIDCO HOUSING RENEWAL & CARPET EXPANSION

Scenario: Redevelopment of Amalgamated CIDCO Sector in Vashi

Three adjacent cooperative housing societies comprising CIDCO buildings built in 1986 (38 years old) amalgamate into a single contiguous plot of 6,000 sq.m (0.60 Hectares) abutting an 18.0 m road. An existing resident owns a flat measuring 400 sq.ft carpet area. Calculate the project FSI and the resident’s statutory new carpet area.

Step 1: Check Age & Road Eligibility under Reg 10.10.2
  • Building Age: 38 years ($> 30\text{ years}$ threshold). Automatically eligible under Reg 10.10.2 without requiring a dilapidation order.
  • Consolidated Plot: $6,000\text{ sq.m} \ge 1,000\text{ sq.m}$.
  • Abutting Road: $18.0\text{ m} \ge 15.0\text{ m}$.
  • Permissible FSI under Table 10-C (i) = 3.00.
  • Total Project Gross BUA: $6,000\text{ sq.m} \times 3.00 = \mathbf{18,000\text{ sq.m}}$.
Step 2: Calculate Flat Owner's Base Rehab Carpet (Clause 2.A.a)
  • Existing Carpet Area: $400.00\text{ sq.ft}$.
  • Base Statutory Expansion: $+35\%$.
  • Base Rehab Entitlement = $400 \times 1.35 = \mathbf{540.00\text{ sq.ft carpet}}$. (Exceeds the $300\text{ sq.ft}$ minimum).
Step 3: Calculate Cluster Amalgamation Bonus (Table 10-D)
  • Amalgamated plot area = $6,000\text{ sq.m}$ (Falls into "Above 4,000 sq.m to 2 Hectares" bracket).
  • Additional Cluster Entitlement = +10% of existing carpet area.
  • Cluster Bonus = $400\text{ sq.ft} \times 10\% = \mathbf{40.00\text{ sq.ft carpet}}$.
Step 4: Final Re-accommodated Flat Area
  • Total Carpet Entitlement = $540\text{ sq.ft (Base)} + 40\text{ sq.ft (Cluster)} = \mathbf{580.00\text{ sq.ft}}$.
  • The member receives a brand new 580 sq.ft carpet apartment completely free of cost (a net 45% increase in living space).
05 // SCRUTINY WATCHOUTS

Common Pitfalls & Sanction Watchouts

COMMON SANCTION & SCRUTINY PITFALLS IN NAVI MUMBAI & CIDCO

  • Demanding Structural Dilapidation Certificates for 30+ Year Buildings: Municipal engineers wrongly requiring C-1 dangerous building certificates for 32-year-old CIDCO societies. Reg 10.10.2 clearly states: "or a building constructed by CIDCO which is above 30 years of age, irrespective of its status of dilapidation."
  • Failing to Award the 300 sq.ft Minimum Carpet Floor: Calculating 35% on a 200 sq.ft EWS flat yields 270 sq.ft. Providing 270 sq.ft violates the statute—Reg 10.10.2.2(A)(a) guarantees an absolute statutory floor of 300 sq.ft carpet.
  • Applying 15m Road Width Rules to Pre-UDCPR 1.50 FSI Leases: Denying 1.50 base FSI to older CIDCO commercial plots fronting 11m or 12m roads. Note 2 below Reg 10.10.1 expressly grandfathers all prior 1.50 CIDCO leases.
  • Forgetting the +0.50 FSI Potential Bonus: Under Note 3 to Reg 10.10.1, plots with 1.50 base FSI are statutorily entitled to exceed the maximum building potential from Table 6-G by an extra 0.50 FSI.
