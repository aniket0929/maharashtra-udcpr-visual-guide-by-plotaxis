Commercial & Industrial Zones: Permissible Uses & Buffer Envelopes

Master the spatial and statutory rules for commercial hubs and manufacturing zones in Maharashtra: permissible retail and wholesale uses in Commercial Zones (Reg. 4.7), manufacturing categories in Industrial Zones (Reg. 4.8), the crucial 23.0-meter buffer setback from residential zones, and worker housing allowances.

01 // EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Plain-Language Executive Summary

Commercial and Industrial zones are the economic workhorses of Maharashtra's urban centers. Regulations 4.7, 4.8, and 4.9 define their allowable activities, fire and environmental safeguards, and statutory buffer distances.

1. Commercial Zone (C-Zone / CBD) (Reg. 4.7)

In Commercial Zones, all uses permissible in Residential Zone R-2 are allowed, plus intensive commercial activities without residential compatibility restrictions:

  • Malls, multiplexes, department stores, retail shops, and business offices.
  • Star-category hotels, restaurants, bars, and banquet halls.
  • Wholesale markets, commodity trading yards, and warehousing.
  • Hospitals, diagnostic hubs, nursing homes, and research centers.
  • Public utility buildings, transport terminals, and multi-level parking lots.

2. Industrial Zone (I-Zone) (Reg. 4.8)

Industrial Zones encompass fabrication, assembly, processing, and warehousing. Permissible uses include:

  • Service Industries (I-1): Without restrictions on power requirement, employment, or floor area.
  • General & Heavy Industries (I-2, I-3): Permitted subject to MPCB (Maharashtra Pollution Control Board) clearances.
  • Worker Housing Quotas (Reg. 4.8(iii)): Up to 25% of the total proposed built-up area may be utilized for residential quarters of essential staff, watchmen, caretakers, and common facilities like canteens, welfare centers, and bank branches.
  • Logistics & Warehousing: Transport hubs, container depots, cold storages, and industrial godowns.

3. The Mandatory 23.0-Meter Industrial Buffer (Reg. 4.8(ii))

Statutory Separation Rule: A minimum buffer open space / setback of not less than 23.0 meters must be maintained between the boundary of an industrial building/use and any adjoining residential or habitable zone.

  • This 23.0m buffer may include standard marginal distances and abutting road widths.
  • The buffer must be kept within the land falling in the industrial zone (unless construction already occurred).
  • Crucial FSI Relief: The area inside this 23.0m buffer open space shall not be deducted for computation of FSI!
  • Exemption: If the industrial land is utilized entirely for non-polluting industries, IT/ITES, or clean tech, the 23.0m buffer is not required.

4. Loom Industry cum Residential Zone (Reg. 4.9)

Established for traditional textile centers (e.g., Bhiwandi, Malegaon, Solapur, Ichalkaranji), allowing powerlooms and handlooms integrated into residential tenements with power restrictions and fire safety safeguards.

02 // STATUTORY RULE

Statutory Rule & Clause Breakdown

UDCPR Reg. 4.7 – 4.9 STATUTORY PROVISION

"4.8 INDUSTRIAL ZONE: Industries shall include any building or part of a building or structure, in which products or materials of all kinds and properties are fabricated, assembled or processed... ii) Any industry / industries may be permitted. Minimum buffer open space / set back (which may include marginal distance and road width if any) from the boundary of industrial Building / use to residential or habitable zone/ use, shall not be less than 23.0 m... Provided that, the area under such buffer open space / setback shall not be deducted for computation of FSI."

REG. 4.8(ii) // 23.0m BUFFER

Mandatory Separation Strip

Guarantees environmental and acoustic segregation between polluting factory units and residential homes. Includes road width and marginal open spaces.

REG. 4.8(iii) // 25% HOUSING

Worker Quarters Allowance

Authorizes up to 25% of total industrial built-up area for caretaker quarters, worker housing, employee canteens, and on-site bank branches.

04 // WORKED PRACTICAL EXAMPLE

Worked Practical Calculation

Practical Scrutiny Problem: An industrial factory proposal has a total proposed industrial built-up area of 10,000 sq.m on a plot in Pune adjoining a residential layout across an 11.0m wide street.

23.0m BUFFER CHECK
Total required buffer = 23.0 meters.
Abutting road width = 11.0 meters.
Buffer to be kept inside the industrial plot = $23.0 - 11.0 = \mathbf{12.0\text{ meters}}$.
The factory building front margin is set at 12.0m. Under Reg. 4.8(ii), this 12m strip is not deducted from the FSI base area!
WORKER HOUSING ALLOCATION
Maximum permissible residential & ancillary use = $10,000 \times 25\% = \mathbf{2,500\text{ sq.m}}$.
The developer plans 1,800 sq.m of staff dormitories, 400 sq.m worker canteen, and 300 sq.m bank branch. All within the 2,500 sq.m limit.
✓ STATUTORY COMPLIANCE: 23m environmental separation achieved and 25% ancillary cap respected.
05 // SCRUTINY WATCHOUTS

Common Pitfalls & Sanction Watchouts

Pitfall 1: Double-Deducting the 23.0m Buffer from Buildable Plot Area

Junior planners frequently subtract the area of the 23.0m industrial buffer from the gross plot area before calculating permissible FSI. The proviso of Regulation 4.8(ii) strictly prohibits this deduction: the developer gets full FSI potential across the entire plot area!

Pitfall 2: Neglecting MPCB Consent to Establish (CTE) Prior to Building Sanction

For Red and Orange category industries, the Planning Authority will not release Commencement Certificate (CC) until the developer submits Consent to Establish from the Maharashtra Pollution Control Board.
