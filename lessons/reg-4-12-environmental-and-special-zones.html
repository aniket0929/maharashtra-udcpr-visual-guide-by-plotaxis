Environmental Buffers, Hill Slopes & Special Purpose Zones

Master the statutory protection corridors and specialized land use envelopes across Maharashtra: River Protection Belts (Reg. 4.12) with 15m/9m buffers, non-buildable Hill Top - Hill Slope (HTHS) zones with gradients steeper than 1:5, Afforestation forest dwelling norms, Defence boundary clearance radii, and quarrying safety perimeters.

01 // EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Plain-Language Executive Summary

Maharashtra's planning framework designates vital ecological and strategic defense zones that impose stringent development barriers. Regulations 4.12 to 4.25 define which activities are permitted and which are completely barred in these sensitive environments.

1. Green Belt & River Protection Belt (Reg. 4.12)

Lands flanking natural water bodies are placed in the Green Belt / River Protection Belt. Permissible activities are strictly limited to non-polluting and open recreational uses:

  • Agriculture, horticulture, nurseries, and public gardens.
  • Pedestrian walkways, jogging tracks, cycle paths, and boat clubs.
  • River & Nallah Buffers (Reg. 4.12.1(v)): Swimming pools, clubhouses, and recreational structures are permitted only after leaving a clear 15.0m buffer from river banks and 9.0m from nallahs.
  • Integration with Layout Open Space (Reg. 4.12.1(vii)): If an adjoining developable plot touches the green belt, the mandatory 10% Recreational Open Space (ROS) may be placed inside the green belt land after observing the 15m/9m water body buffer.

2. Hill Top - Hill Slope (HTHS) & Afforestation Zones (Reg. 4.16 & 4.17)

  • HTHS Slope Gradient Rule (Reg. 4.2(XIV) & 4.17): Any hill slope having a gradient steeper than 1:5 (vertical to horizontal), irrespective of whether specifically demarcated on the plan or not, is treated as non-buildable hill slope to prevent landslides and soil erosion.
  • Forest Houses in Afforestation Zone (Reg. 4.16(ii)): Permitted for caretakers and tourists on forest plots not less than 0.40 Hectare (4,000 sq.m), with maximum built-up area of 150 sq.m, Ground + 1 floor, height at or below 7.0m, and mandatory planting of 250 trees per hectare.

3. Defence, Quarry & Transport Zones (Reg. 4.13, 4.20, 4.21)

  • Defence Zone (Reg. 4.20): Reserved exclusively for Ministry of Defence facilities. Surrounding private lands fall under restrictive security buffers (typically 100m to 500m per Ministry guidelines) requiring mandatory Defence NOC prior to municipal sanction.
  • Mines & Quarry Zone (Reg. 4.21): Stone crushing and quarrying permissible subject to safe blasting distance buffers (typically 500m from inhabited gaothans and schools) under Regulation 15.1.
  • Traffic & Transportation Zone (Reg. 4.13): Railway and transit hubs. Crucially, separable surplus railway lands are legally permissible to be developed for Commercial Zone uses to fund transit modernization.
02 // STATUTORY RULE

Statutory Rule & Clause Breakdown

UDCPR Reg. 4.12 – 4.25 STATUTORY PROVISION

"4.12 GREEN BELT ZONE / RIVER PROTECTION BELT: Following uses shall be permissible: i) Agriculture, ii) Tree Plantation, Gardens, Public park, Recreational Open Space... v) Swimming pools, club houses, recreational facilities after leaving 15.0 m. belt along river bank and 9.0 m. from Nallahs... 4.16 AFFORESTATION ZONE: ii) Forest houses... not exceeding 150 sq.m., provided forest plot is not less than 0.4 hectare, ground + 1 floor, height not more than 7.0 m... 4.13 TRAFFIC AND TRANSPORTATION ZONE: ii) Separable lands of Railways shall be allowed to be developed for uses permissible in Commercial Zone."

REG. 4.12 // RIVER BUFFER

15.0m River / 9.0m Nallah

Mandates permanent non-buildable setback along watercourses. Recreational clubhouses and pools are permitted only outside this 15.0m boundary.

REG. 4.17 // HTHS 1:5 GRADIENT

Slope Stability Safeguard

Slopes steeper than 1:5 gradient are non-buildable whether colored on DP or not. Prevents slope cutting, foundation failure, and ecological degradation.

04 // WORKED PRACTICAL EXAMPLE

Worked Practical Calculation

Practical Scrutiny Problem: An architect is hired to plan a resort clubhouse on a land parcel in Karjat bordering a river. The building height is designed at 12.0 meters.

RIVER BUFFER AUDIT
Under Regulation 4.12.1(v) & (c), the setback from the river bank must be the greater of:
1. Mandatory statutory river buffer = 15.0 meters.
2. Building height setback ($H/5$) = $12.0 / 5 = \mathbf{2.40\text{ meters}}$.
3. Offset from Green Belt boundary = 4.50 meters.
SITE LAYOUT PLACEMENT
The 15.0-meter statutory river buffer governs as the strictest constraint.
The 15.0m riverfront strip is designed as open lawns and tree grove. The clubhouse foundation starts exactly at 15.01m from the river bank edge.
✓ STATUTORY RESULT: Siting approved under Reg. 4.12.1(v) with zero ecological encroachment.
05 // SCRUTINY WATCHOUTS

Common Pitfalls & Sanction Watchouts

Pitfall 1: Leveling Steep Hill Slopes Without Slope Gradient Certification

Even if an area is colored as general development zone on an older regional plan, if the physical site slope exceeds a 1:5 gradient, Regulation 4.2(XIV) and 4.16(vi) classify it as non-buildable HTHS. Building on it exposes the developer to criminal action under the Environment Protection Act.

Pitfall 2: Confusing Green Belt 15m Buffer with Normal Side Margins

Normal low-rise buildings require only 2.25m or 3.0m side margins. However, whenever a building abuts a river, the 15.0m Green Belt buffer overrides standard marginal distances.
